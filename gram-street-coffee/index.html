OUR ETHOS & ROASTERY

Built on relationships, not contracts.

Gram Street Coffee was born out of an obsession with the micro-lot: the small, overlooked five-acre farm parcels in the hills of Nagaland and the shaded canopies of Karnataka that standard commercial roasters overlook.

We don't buy from anonymous commodity brokers. Every season, our roaster travels directly to partner estates, cupping side-by-side with producers, funding raised African drying beds, and securing experimental anaerobic fermentations before they hit open auctions.

In our Arjun Marg roastery, beans are roasted in small 6kg batches on a cast-iron Giesen drum, profiling each crop’s thermal curve to unlock fruit sweetness rather than burnt bitterness. Rested for 14 days, never rushed.
