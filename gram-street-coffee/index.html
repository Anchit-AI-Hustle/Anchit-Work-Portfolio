PROVENANCE & PRACTICE

Built on relationships, not contracts.

Gram Street Coffee began with a simple frustration: coffee in Gurugram was treated either as convenience fuel or a sugary vehicle for syrups. We wanted a space that approached extraction with the nuance of wine.

We partner directly with smallholder farming families across the misty hills of Nagaland and the shade-grown canopies of Chikmagalur. We pay well above fair-trade premiums so growers can experiment with extended anaerobic fermentations and raised-bed natural drying.

In our DLF Phase 1 roastery, every roast profile is calibrated in 5-kilogram batches, tracking rate of rise and Agtron bean coloration to highlight origin terroir rather than roast bitterness.
