The Sensory Craft

Three Disciplines.

Every pull, roast, and whisk at Gram Street is treated as an exact culinary science.

01. Drum Roasting

First Crack Alchemy

204.5°C thermodynamic curve developed in Gurugram to unlock dark cocoa and roasted hazelnut.

02. 9-Bar Extraction

Liquid Velvet Crema

Extracted on high-polish chrome group heads with 93.5°C pre-infusion for dense tiger-striping.

03. Kyoto Uji Whisk

100-Prong Chasen Aeration

First-harvest Tencha stone-ground in Kyoto, whisked at 78°C into silky jade micro-foam.
