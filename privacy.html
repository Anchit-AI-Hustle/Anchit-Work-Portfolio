Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This is a personal portfolio run by one person. It is not a company, it sells nothing, and it has no advertising. This page describes exactly what the site stores and what leaves your browser — no more and no less.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up and no tracking profile is needed to read anything here.
  • One cookie is set, and only to remember whether you collapsed the sidebar.
  • If you use the chatbot, what you type is sent to an AI provider to produce a reply.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared for advertising.

Who is responsible

Anchit Tandon, Delhi, India. For anything on this page, including a request to delete something, write to anchit.tandon@gmail.com.

Cookies

This site sets exactly one cookie:

NamePurposeLifetime
sb_collapsed Remembers whether you collapsed the navigation sidebar, so the layout is the same when you come back. 1 year

It holds a single digit, 0 or 1. It contains no identifier, it is not read by anyone else, and it is set SameSite=Lax so it is not sent from other sites. Because it only stores a display choice you made yourself, it is a strictly functional cookie and does not require consent.

Things stored in your browser

The site also uses your browser's local storage for preferences. This data never leaves your device and is not readable by this site's server:

KeyWhat it remembers
anchit-themeLight or dark theme
anchit-motionWhether you turned animation off
anchit-sidebar-collapsedSidebar state
anchit-visitedWhether you have been here before, so the intro is not replayed
anchit-chat-mode, anchit-chat-audio, anchit-tts-local, jarvis-voiceYour chat and voice settings
anchit-chat-feedbackAny thumbs up or down you gave a chat reply
cookie-consentYour answer to the consent banner, so it is not shown again

Clearing site data in your browser removes all of it.

The chatbot and voice features

The chat on this site is answered by a large language model. When you send a message, the text of that message and the recent messages in the same conversation are sent to this site's own serverless function, which forwards them to an AI provider (Anthropic, and where that is unavailable, Google, Groq, Cerebras or OpenRouter) purely to generate the reply. If you use the spoken replies, the reply text is sent to a text-to-speech provider.

I do not store a transcript of your conversation on any server I control. Do not type anything confidential into it — it is a demo on a portfolio, not a secure channel.

Analytics

If, and only if, you accept analytics in the consent banner, the site loads a privacy-preserving analytics script that records page views. It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint your device, and does not build a cross-site profile. Decline, and the script is never loaded at all.

You can change your mind at any time using the button at the bottom of this page.

Contact forms

Where this site offers a form, what you type is used only to reply to you. It is not added to a mailing list.

Other people's websites

Pages here link to live projects and third-party sites, and some embed fonts from Google Fonts. Once you follow a link or load an embedded resource, that provider's own privacy policy applies. I have no control over what they collect.

Your rights

You can ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to its processing. In practice there is very little to hold: delete your browser's site data and almost nothing remains. For anything else, email anchit.tandon@gmail.com and I will answer within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
