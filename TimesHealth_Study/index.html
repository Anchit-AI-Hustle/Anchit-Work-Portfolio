Why people stop using Times Health+, and how to fix it

What it is
  • Times Health+: paid live online yoga classes, at 8 fixed times a day.
  • Diet plans for PCOS, diabetes, blood pressure and more, sold separately.
  • The Times Internet Half Marathon, now a four-city series.
What goes wrong
  • You cannot see the price until you give your phone number.
  • Miss your class time and there is no catch-up, so people drop off.
  • People with a health condition are sent to a different product.
  • Runners get nothing from Times Health after race day.
What I would do
  • Show the price and class times on the first screen.
  • Record every class, and send a short catch-up after a missed day.
  • 12-week programmes for diabetes, BP, PCOS and back pain.
  • A training plan for every runner, then a next goal.

The fix is one habit loop across all three: see the price, pick a time that fits your day, practise live or on catch-up, get a weekly check-in tied to your goal, and, for runners, a training plan that starts at registration and a next goal after the finish line. Fixes F1, F2, F4 and F5 need no new content.

Built by me to show the fixes working together. Demo data only; nothing is stored or sent, and the prices are illustrative.

What you can try

  • Start: pick a goal, your level and when you are free, and see the plan, the price and your matched class times before any sign-up (F1, F4, F6).
  • My week: mark classes done live, on catch-up or missed, and watch the streak, grace days and the rescue nudge respond (F3, F5).
  • Race plan: choose a city and distance, say if it is your first race, and get a week-by-week plan to race day with yoga for runners (F10, F11).
  • Business view: how many runners become members, with every assumption on a slider.
