What works

A credible partner in The Yoga Institute, named teachers, live human classes eight times a day, and a diet product that already covers the conditions people pay for.

What hurts

The page loads from 49 hosts, including several ad pixels and two session recorders, and the tag manager blocks the main thread for 5.5 s. On a mid-range phone that is the difference between a trial and a bounce.

What is missing

A price, a refund page, a catch-up option and any path between the three products. These cost little to add and are what the fixes start with.

Times Health+ will not win on price or on the size of its library. It can win on outcomes for a condition, backed by The Yoga Institute and a diet team, and on the race-to-habit path no rival owns. Both need the pieces it already has joined up.
