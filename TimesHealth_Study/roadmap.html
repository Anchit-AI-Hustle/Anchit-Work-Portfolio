Roadmap

From idea to scale: how each bet gets built, launched, marketed and grown

Each of the three big bets, carried through six stages: the idea, the first build, the launch, the marketing, the scaling, and the upgrades after. Every stage has a measure and a gate, and every gate leads back to one number: members who practise three or more days in their fourth week.

Days 1 to 90Now: fix and prove

  • Ship F1, F2, F7 and F9, then catch-up (fix F3) and the slot matcher (fix F4)
  • Weekly check-in (fix F5) for half of new members
  • The race plan (fix F10) for Mumbai; design the first condition track (fix F6)

Months 4 to 9Next: roll out what passed

  • Prediabetes pilot: 200 users, 12 weeks, then open to all
  • Practise to pay less, built and tested on new trials
  • Race plan in all four cities, with recovery after

Months 10 to 18Later: grow and extend

  • Blood pressure, then PCOS and back pain programmes
  • Series passport; practise to pay less for all new members
  • Doctors, employers and insurers as routes in

Days 1 to 90 are the plan's first 90 days. Later months are a sequence, not a promise: each step starts only when the one before passes its gate. Every "Stop if" line comes from the study; later gates are assumptions to test.

Big bet 1

Outcome programmes: pay for a result, not a class

Built on: Big bet 1 on the Ideas page · The plan · The fixes · See it in the working model (Start) →

1
IdeaThe problem, the insight and the bet
Problems
P3 segment mismatch; P5 low social proof
Insight
23.7 crore Indians have diabetes or prediabetes. Diabetes coaching apps track numbers, but none is built around yoga. Times Health has a diet team for 16+ conditions and The Yoga Institute's teachers, and sells them apart.
The bet
12-week programmes for one condition at a time: yoga and food together, with one number tracked.
Hypothesis
If users with prediabetes get yoga and food together, with one number to track, more of them complete 12 weeks and their HbA1c improves more than a waitlist group's.
2
BuildWhat the first version holds, who builds it, and when
First version
  • A safety screen that sends users who need a doctor to one first
  • The prediabetes track: the right classes and a weekly diet consult (fix F6)
  • A lab HbA1c test (average blood sugar over about three months) at the start and at week 12
  • A weekly log of practice and food, and a one-page report at week 12 to share with a doctor
Not yet
  • Blood pressure, PCOS and back pain wait until prediabetes passes its gate
Team
A product manager, a medical reviewer, the diet team lead, a programme lead from The Yoga Institute, two engineers, and legal and ethics review.
When
Designed in days 61 to 90, as the plan sets out; the pilot runs 12 weeks from month 4.
Depends on
Ethics review before any outcome is published; a lab partner for the HbA1c tests; consent for health data.
3
LaunchHow it reaches users, and the line that stops it
How
One programme, prediabetes, with 200 users for 12 weeks, a medical reviewer and a waitlist group (users who start later) for comparison. Report the result honestly, whatever it shows.
Rollout
  1. 1Pilot: 200 users, 12 weeks
  2. 2Open to all users with prediabetes, if the gate is passed
  3. 3The next condition, with its own pilot

Stop if: fewer than 40% of the pilot complete the 12 weeks, or if the typical HbA1c change is no better than the waitlist group's.

4
MarketWho it is for, what we say, and where
Position
A 12-week programme for prediabetes, with one number to show your doctor.
For
Readers of TOI health stories, and members whose goal is blood sugar.
Message
"Track one number for 12 weeks." No promise of results, ever.
Channels
  • A 30-second quiz on TOI health stories that routes to the right programme (idea T10)
  • The price-first Start page (fix F1)
  • Later, doctors' referral codes (idea T16)
Moment
Publishing the pilot's result, with its method, is the launch.
Price
A programme price for 12 weeks, above general yoga, because it sells a result.
5
ScalePhases, each with a gate to pass first
  1. Months 7 to 9Prediabetes open to allGate: Completion at least 40%, and HbA1c better than the waitlist
  2. Months 10 to 18Blood pressure, then PCOS and back painGate: Each condition passes its own pilot first
Run it
Diet consult capacity planned per condition, a medical reviewer for each, and a legal review of every claim against India's advertising rules.
6
UpgradesWhat comes after, from ideas already scored
Version 2
  • Doctor referral: diabetologists give patients a code for the programme (idea T16)
  • Wearable sync: steps and sleep fill in the weekly check-in (idea T14)
Version 3
  • Insurer-funded programmes, where classes count as active days (idea T5)
  • Parents' plan: gentle chair yoga for users over 60, paid by a child (idea T12)

Planned, not built. Each comes from an idea already scored on the Ideas page, or joins two big bets.

Big bet 2

Race to habit: the series passport

Built on: Big bet 2 on the Ideas page · The plan · The fixes · See it in the working model (Race plan) →

1
IdeaThe problem, the insight and the bet
Problems
P6 post-event drop-off; P8 event-side friction and safety risk
Insight
The Delhi race had 15,000+ runners, by my launch figure, against 40,000+ at the long-established Delhi Half Marathon. A new series will not win on size. It can win on the weeks before and after race day.
The bet
A training plan from the day you register, recovery after, and the next city as your goal.
Hypothesis
If registrants get a plan from day one and a recovery programme after, more of them start a yoga trial and register for the next city.
2
BuildWhat the first version holds, who builds it, and when
First version
  • An opt-in at registration, with its own consent
  • A week-by-week plan by distance, gentler for first-timers, held back when the runway is too short (fix F10)
  • The heat and air-quality plan for race week (fix F14)
  • Results, photos and the certificate the same day, then a 30-day recovery programme (fix F11)
Not yet
  • The series passport and the corporate league wait for the second season
Team
A product manager, the events lead, a running coach, a yoga-for-runners lead, two engineers, and a messaging lead.
When
Days 61 to 90, in time for the Mumbai race on 14 Feb 2027; runners with too few weeks before the race get the gentler plan, or none, as the working model does.
Depends on
A separate opt-in, since registration data is collected for the race; the registration platform; a plan that survives a rescheduled race.
3
LaunchHow it reaches users, and the line that stops it
How
The Mumbai race on 14 Feb 2027: offer the plan to half of registrants. Measure plan starts, trial starts and repeat registrations against the other half.
Rollout
  1. 1Mumbai: half of registrants
  2. 2All four cities, if the gate is passed
  3. 3Returning runners get the passport

Stop if: fewer than 20% of registrants start the plan, or if fewer than 5% of plan users start a yoga trial.

4
MarketWho it is for, what we say, and where
Position
From registration to race day, and after.
For
Registrants, with first-timers flagged at sign-up, and past finishers.
Message
"Your plan starts the day you register."
Channels
  • The registration confirmation
  • Race-week messages with the heat and air plan
  • The results page, the same day
  • Company HR teams, for team entries
Moment
Registration opening for each city, and the finish line.
Price
The plan is free. A returning-runner price for the next city (idea T6) and a yoga trial.
5
ScalePhases, each with a gate to pass first
  1. Months 4 to 9All four cities, with recovery after each raceGate: Plan starts at least 20%; trials at least 5% of plan users
  2. Months 10 to 18Series passport and returning-runner price (idea T6)Gate: Repeat registrations rise; new members from runners are compared with the working model's estimate of 150 to 1,200 a year, an assumption to test
Run it
A city calendar with safety rules per city, coaching content reviewed by the running coach, and one Times Health login for race and yoga (fix F13).
6
UpgradesWhat comes after, from ideas already scored
Version 2
  • Smog-day plan: on poor-air days, training switches to indoor yoga and strength (idea T17)
  • Corporate league: company teams across the four cities, with yoga seats (idea T8)
Version 3
  • Wearable sync, so a runner's steps and sleep shape the training weeks (idea T14)
  • Big-screen mode for runners' yoga at home (idea T11)

Big bet 3

Practise to pay less

Built on: Big bet 3 on the Ideas page · The plan · The fixes · See it in the working model (My week) →

1
IdeaThe problem, the insight and the bet
Problems
P2 schedule rigidity
Insight
Users pay for exercise they do not do: gym members on flat fees went 4.3 times a month. Linking money to the behaviour helps, and a reward is gentler than a deposit.
The bet
Practise 12 times in your first month and part of the fee comes back as credit.
Hypothesis
If new members earn credit by practising 12 times in month one, more of them practise in week four and renew in month two, at a cost below the renewals it adds.
2
BuildWhat the first version holds, who builds it, and when
First version
  • One plain sentence at sign-up: practise 12 times and get part of the fee back as credit
  • A "7 of 12" tracker that counts live and catch-up classes
  • A 20-minute catch-up after two missed days (fixes F3 and F5)
  • Credit applied at the next renewal, with a cap
Not yet
  • Live shout-outs and a weekly streak card on WhatsApp (idea T9) come next
Team
A product manager, two engineers, billing and finance, a messaging lead, and instructors for shout-outs later.
When
Months 4 to 5, after catch-up for every class (fix F3) is live from days 31 to 60, because the target must be reachable.
Depends on
Catch-up for every class (fix F3); billing that can apply a credit; terms with no fine print.
3
LaunchHow it reaches users, and the line that stops it
How
A randomised test on new trials: half get the offer, half do not. Measure practice days in week four, renewal in month two, and the cost of credits.
Rollout
  1. 1Half of new trials
  2. 2All new members, if the gate is passed
  3. 3Target and credit tuned by plan

Stop if: the extra renewal money is less than the credits cost, over two months of new members.

4
MarketWho it is for, what we say, and where
Position
The plan that pays you back for showing up.
For
New trial starters, especially busy members who fear missing classes.
Message
"Practise 12 times this month and part of your fee comes back."
Channels
  • The price-first page (fix F1) and sign-up
  • The tracker in the app
  • A weekly streak card on WhatsApp (idea T9)
Moment
Every sign-up: the offer is part of the price, not a campaign.
Price
A capped credit, set below the value of the renewals it adds.
5
ScalePhases, each with a gate to pass first
  1. Months 9 to 12All new members, after the test on months 5 to 8 of new trialsGate: Renewal money above the cost of credits
  2. Months 13 to 18Target and credit tuned for monthly and yearly plans; ET Prime's three free months included (idea T13)Gate: Week-four practice rises without credit cost rising faster
Run it
Credit owed tracked monthly by finance; only real practice counts, checked against class attendance.
6
UpgradesWhat comes after, from ideas already scored
Version 2
  • Live shout-outs in class and a weekly streak card (idea T9)
  • Big-screen mode: cast classes to the TV (idea T11)
Version 3
  • A guided first week for ET Prime's three free months (idea T13)
  • A price-first page that shows the credit beside every plan (idea T1)
