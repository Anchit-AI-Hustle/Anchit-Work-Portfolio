Times Health Study/Plan
How I would know it works

One number to steer by, and the first 90 days

The one number

Members who practise three or more days in their fourth week. Renewal follows the habit, and the habit is decided in the first month. Trials can grow while week four stays empty; this number cannot.

Early signals

  • Landing view to trial start (F1).
  • Share of practice done on catch-up (F3).
  • Race registrants who start the training plan (F10).
  • Condition-programme members whose number moved (F6).

Guardrails

  • Refund requests per 100 conversions.
  • Live-class attendance does not fall as catch-up grows.
  • No health claim without a published method.

My first 90 days

Days 1 to 30Make it easy to say yes

F1 price and times up front, F2 one refund and trial page, F7 the performance budget, F9 the facts checklist. Baseline the week-four number for the last three monthly cohorts.

Days 31 to 60Make it easy to stay

F3 catch-up for every class and F4 the slot matcher. Turn on the weekly check-in (F5) for half of new members, with the other half as a holdout.

Days 61 to 90Join the products up

F10 for the Mumbai race on 14 Feb 2027, 15 weeks out: a training plan for every registrant, and a recovery programme after. Design the first condition track (F6) with the diet team and a medical reviewer.
