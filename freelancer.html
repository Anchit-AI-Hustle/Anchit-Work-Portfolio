AI TeleSuite · Reviewing sales calls

Professional work / Times Internet

I built a tool that writes up every sales call, scores how it went and suggests what to say next — so a manager can review calls without sitting through all of them.

Read case study: AI TeleSuite

The problem

Sales calls are full of useful information, but nobody has time to listen to all of them.

My contribution

It writes up the call, scores it against what the team agreed good looks like, and suggests what to say next. A manager can read a call in a minute instead of listening to the whole thing.

The useful pattern

Start with one job, and agree up front what a good call sounds like. Keep the write-up readable, keep a person checking it, and make sure it leads to something the team actually does next.
