Why ET Prime members are unhappy, and how to fix it

1 · Who the users are
  • Daily readers of business news, mostly free
  • Retail investors in ET Markets
  • Paying members of ET Prime
  • Team buyers on group plans
2 · Why they come, and what they want
  • Fast, trusted business news
  • What the news means for their own stocks
  • Depth without ads or hassle
  • Intelligence for their teams
3 · What they get today
  • Slow pages crowded with ads
  • A general feed; the portfolio tracker removed
  • Ads, pop-ups, sales calls and some lock-outs
  • Group plans sold like single plans
4 · What is missing
  • A reason to return daily that does not depend on Google
  • News explained for their holdings
  • A paid experience clearly better than free, and self-serve billing
  • A product built for teams

Issues likely hurting conversion and retention

The 8 problems, most serious first

  1. P1Member experience leakageUsers who have paid still see ads, pop-ups for the plan they already own, and get sales calls.
  2. P2Access and billing failuresSome paying members are locked out of what they bought, charged twice, or have to email to get an invoice.
  3. P3Offer sprawl and price inconsistencyThere are many offers at once, and the website, the apps and TOI+ contradict each other on price and on what is included.
  4. P4Low paid conversion and an audience gapET has half of Moneycontrol's audience and a much smaller paying base, at a much higher entry price.
  5. P5Page performance and ad densityThe mobile site is heavy, slow to load and crowded with ads, starting above the first headline.
  6. P6Acquisition concentration riskA large share of new readers arrive from Google, and Google is sending fewer every year.
  7. P7Feature regression and data trustET Markets removed a portfolio tracker some investors had used for years, and some report prices that are not up to date.
  8. P8Notification routing and alert fatigueTapping a notification can open the home page instead of the story, and readers get more alerts than they asked for.

Try a member-first ET: My Stocks, the member hub and the rules

Fictional demo data; nothing is stored or sent.

My Stocks →Member hub →Who sees what →

What you can try

  • My Stocks: pick what you hold and the front page becomes your stocks, with "what it means for you" and a morning brief (fixes F13 and F11).
  • Member hub: restore access in one tap, download an invoice, cancel auto-renew in two taps, and see the renewal reminders and your value receipt (fixes F3, F4 and F14).
  • Who sees what: switch between a new visitor, a reader, a member, a lapsed member and someone who asked for a call, and see which ads, offers and sales calls the rules allow (fixes F1, F2, F5 and F8).
  • Renewal maths: what each one-point rise in renewals is worth, with every input on a slider.
