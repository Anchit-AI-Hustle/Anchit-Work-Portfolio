Why ET Prime members are unhappy, and how to fix it

What it is
  • The Economic Times: business news, on the web and in an app.
  • ET Markets: the stock market app and section.
  • ET Prime: the paid membership, ₹2,699 for 12 months on the web.
What goes wrong
  • Paying members still get ads, pop-ups and sales calls.
  • Some are locked out, charged twice or cannot get an invoice.
  • Prices and offers contradict each other.
  • Pages are slow on phones, and Google sends fewer readers each year.
What I would do
  • Never show ads, pop-ups or sales calls to someone who has paid.
  • Self-serve access, invoices and cancelling, in one or two taps.
  • One price list, one offer at a time.
  • A "My Stocks" front page built around what you own.

The key numbers

Four numbers that explain the problem

ET Prime subscribers~1.75 lakhCrisil, June 2025, the last independent count. The plans page claims "10 Lakh+ members", which I could not reconcile. Moneycontrol Pro announced 10 lakh+ paying subscribers in October 2024.
Audience vs Moneycontrol31.4M vs 63.8MUnique visitors, Comscore February 2026; minutes 129.7M vs 546.3M.
Requests to load the mobile home page1,364From 285 hosts, before the load event fired at 25.5 s (my audit, 390 px, 1 Nov 2026).
Google search referrals to news sites-40.2%July 2025 to July 2026, Chartbeat; Discover -34.3%. Search was 38.8% of ET's referrals (Semrush estimate).

The fix is not another offer. It is a member-first ET: one entitlement, one price book, no ads or sales calls for people who have paid, and a reason to open ET every day that does not depend on Google. Fixes F1 to F4 cost little and can ship inside 90 days.

Working prototype

Try a member-first ET: My Stocks, the member hub and the rules

Built by me to show the fixes working together. Companies, stories and prices in it are fictional demo data; nothing is stored or sent.

What you can try

  • My Stocks: pick what you hold and the front page becomes your stocks, with "what it means for you" and a morning brief (F13, F11).
  • Member hub: restore access in one tap, download an invoice, cancel auto-renew in two taps, and see the renewal reminders and your value receipt (F3, F4, F14).
  • Who sees what: switch between a new visitor, a reader, a member, a lapsed member and someone who asked for a call, and see which ads, offers and sales calls the rules allow (F1, F2, F5, F8).
  • Renewal maths: what each point of renewal is worth, with every input on a slider.
