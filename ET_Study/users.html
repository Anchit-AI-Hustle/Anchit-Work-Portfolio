Users

Who uses ET, what they want, what they get, and what is missing

The daily reader

1 · Who they are
Professionals and students who read business news on a phone, often arriving from Google or a notification. Most do not pay.
2 · Why they come, and what they want
To know what matters in business and markets today, quickly, from a source they trust.
3 · What they get today
A page that keeps loading for 25 seconds, with an empty ad space above the first headline, and notifications that can open the home page instead of the story.
4 · What is missing
A fast page, notifications that open the story, and a short daily brief worth coming back for.

Sources: My live audit, 1 Nov 2026 · Review: notification opens the home page

The retail investor

1 · Who they are
One of India's 13.1 crore investors, holding stocks or funds, checking ET Markets during market hours.
2 · Why they come, and what they want
To know what the news means for the stocks they own, with prices they can trust and tools that keep their data.
3 · What they get today
A general markets feed not linked to their holdings. The portfolio tracker was removed around June 2026, with no export according to reviewers, and some report prices that are not up to date.
4 · What is missing
News explained for their own holdings, reliable prices, and an easy way to bring in their portfolio.

Sources: NSE unique investors, May 2026 · Review: portfolio removed

The paying member

1 · Who they are
About 1.75 lakh ET Prime members on the last independent count (Crisil, June 2025), many on one- or two-year plans.
2 · Why they come, and what they want
Deep, original reporting and investor tools, without ads or interruptions, with billing that just works.
3 · What they get today
Ads and pop-ups for the plan they already own, sales calls, some lock-outs, and invoices only by emailing the care desk.
4 · What is missing
An experience clearly better than the free one, and self-serve access, invoices and cancelling.

Sources: Crisil rating report, June 2025 · ET app reviews, Google Play

The team buyer

1 · Who they are
Companies and colleges that buy ET Prime for a group, through the corporate and campus plans on the plans page.
2 · Why they come, and what they want
Business and sector intelligence for their teams, easy to buy, share and manage.
3 · What they get today
Group plans listed on the plans page and sold much like single plans.
4 · What is missing
Seats, simple admin, and sector briefs made for teams.

Sources: ET Prime plans and FAQ

What ET needs from these users

Members who renew because they use ET every week, investors who make ET part of their routine, and readers who come back on their own rather than through Google.
