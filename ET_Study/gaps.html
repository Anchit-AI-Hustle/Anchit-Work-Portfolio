Gap analysis

Where ET falls short of what readers need, and of its rivals

ET does not need a cheaper plan. It needs readers who come back.Moneycontrol Pro costs about a fifth of ET Prime, yet at list prices the two collect similar subscription money. Moneycontrol gets there with twice the monthly audience, a higher paying share and readers who spend twice as long. Copying its price would not close the gap; more readers returning every week would. Whether price explains the difference in paying share is untested, so it should be tested, not assumed.

Some of what this page looks at was built while I worked at Times Internet. It reviews the product as a new visitor sees it today, from public sources only.
