Gap analysis

Where ET falls short of what readers need, and of its rivals

ET does not need a cheaper plan. It needs readers who come back.Moneycontrol Pro costs about a fifth of ET Prime, yet at list prices the two collect similar subscription money. Moneycontrol gets there with twice the monthly audience, a larger share of readers who pay and readers who spend twice as long. Copying its price would not close the gap; more readers returning every week would. Whether price explains why more of its readers pay is untested, so it should be tested, not assumed.

Some of what this page looks at was built while I worked at Times Internet. It reviews the product as a new visitor sees it today, from public sources only.

Where users drop off

3. From first visit to loyal member, step by step

Every reader goes through five steps: Reach (finding ET), Engage (reading), Convert (paying), Keep (staying a member) and Expand (adding more, such as seats for a team). Each card shows where readers drop off at that step, and which ideas fix it.

1 · ReachGoogle brings readers, and is sending fewerSearch referrals to publishers fell 40.2% in a year; a Semrush estimate has ET's visits down from 64.6M in July to 49.2M in September 2026 (cause not isolated)Fixed by: Ask ET, morning brief, lite mode (ideas I5, I14 and I15)
2 · EngageReaders skim and leave4.1 minutes a month per visitor, against 8.6 at MoneycontrolFixed by: My Portfolio, Earnings Day Live (ideas I2 and I4)
3 · ConvertFew readers pay0.56% of visitors pay, against 1.6%; twelve buttons in one paywall; offers that contradict each otherFixed by: One price ladder (idea I8), gift a month (idea I10)
4 · KeepPaying members are still sold to1 in 5 recent negative reviews: ads or sales pitches after paying; lock-outs and billing errorsFixed by: Member mode, pause, Investor Wrapped (ideas I1, I12 and I3)
5 · ExpandNo step up, no seats for teamsNo higher plan to move up to on the web; corporate plans sold but not a productFixed by: Price ladder (idea I8), ET Prime for Teams (idea I9)
