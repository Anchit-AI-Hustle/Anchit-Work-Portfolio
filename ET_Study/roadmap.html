Roadmap

From idea to scale: how each bet gets built, launched, marketed and grown

Each of the three big bets, carried through six stages: the idea, the first build, the launch, the marketing, the scaling, and the upgrades after. Every stage has a measure and a gate, and every gate leads back to one number: members who use ET three or more days a week.

Days 1 to 90Now: fix and prove

  • Ship F1, F5 and F11, then F2, F3, F4 and F9
  • Closed beta of My Stocks (F13) with 5,000 members
  • Randomised test of a web entry tier (F7)

Months 4 to 9Next: roll out what passed

  • My Portfolio for all members, with results-day summaries
  • Receipt, Wrapped and pause built, then tested from month 7
  • Ask ET on 10% of article pages, then every article page

Months 10 to 18Later: grow and extend

  • Free readers follow one holding
  • Receipt, Wrapped and pause for all, if the test passes
  • Ask ET on WhatsApp; archive licensing once rights are clear

Days 1 to 90 are the plan's first 90 days. Later months are a sequence, not a promise: each step starts only when the one before passes its gate. Every "Stop if" line comes from the study; later gates are assumptions to test.

Big bet 1

My Portfolio: ET around what you own

Built on: Big bet 1 on the Ideas page · The plan · The fixes · See it in the working model (My Stocks) →

1
IdeaThe problem, the insight and the bet
Problems
P4, P6, P7
Insight
Readers spend 4.1 minutes a month on ET against 8.6 on Moneycontrol. Rivals show headlines next to holdings; none explains what a story means for a reader's own position. ET has the newsroom and ET Now to do it.
The bet
Import holdings once; every story, result and TV clip about them comes to you, explained.
Hypothesis
If members see every story about their own stocks explained for them, they use ET on more days each week, and they renew.
2
BuildWhat the first version holds, who builds it, and when
First version
  • Import from the depository statement (CAS) the member already receives, with clear consent and one-tap deletion
  • Each stock mapped to the ET stories already tagged with it
  • A "what it means for you" line, written and checked by the markets desk, starting with the most-held companies
  • The feed on the web and both apps (F13)
Not yet
  • Results-day summaries (I4) and ET Now clips (I11) wait for the second release
Team
A product manager, a designer, three engineers, a data engineer for the stock mapping, two markets editors, and a legal reviewer.
When
Days 1 to 60, so the closed beta can start in days 61 to 90, as the plan sets out.
Depends on
Legal sign-off that the line explains and never recommends, under SEBI's rules; consent and deletion under India's data protection law; good story tagging.
3
LaunchHow it reaches users, and the line that stops it
How
A closed beta with 5,000 members, compared with 5,000 who do not get it, for 8 weeks. Measure days active a week and renewal intent.
Rollout
  1. 1Closed beta: 5,000 members
  2. 2All members, if the gate is passed
  3. 3Free readers follow one holding, to taste it

Stop if: fewer than 15% of invited members import within 30 days, or if those who import are not active at least 15% more days a week than the matched group.

4
MarketWho it is for, what we say, and where
Position
The only place that explains what the news means for your own stocks.
For
Members first, because this is a renewal product. Then retail investors among free readers.
Message
"Import once. Every story about your stocks, explained."
Channels
  • A "follow this stock" button on every stock page and story
  • Member emails and the monthly value receipt
  • ET Now segments that point viewers to their own feed
Moment
The next quarterly results season, when Earnings Day Live (I4) shows the value fastest.
Price
Inside ET Prime. Free readers can follow one holding; members follow all of them.
5
ScalePhases, each with a gate to pass first
  1. Months 4 to 9All members, after the beta passes; results-day summaries (I4) and ET Now clips (I11) addedGate: Weekly engaged members rise against the matched group
  2. Months 10 to 18Free readers follow one holding, with Prime to follow allGate: Prime starts from followers; a target to set after the beta, not before
Run it
Explainer coverage grows from the most-held companies outward, using templates the desk checks. Monthly audits of consent and deletion. Cost per active member tracked from day one.
6
UpgradesWhat comes after, from ideas already scored
Version 2
  • Market-hours mode: live data 9:15 to 3:30, reading mode after the close (I7)
  • A WhatsApp morning brief on your own stocks, for those who opt in (I14)
Version 3
  • ET inside the broker: Prime explainers on a broker's holdings screen, for a share of revenue (I16)
  • One price ladder, with My Portfolio as the heart of Prime Pro (I8)

Planned, not built. Each comes from an idea already scored on the Ideas page, or joins two big bets.

Big bet 2

Renew by value: member mode, Investor Wrapped and pause

Built on: Big bet 2 on the Ideas page · The plan · The fixes · See it in the working model (Member hub) →

1
IdeaThe problem, the insight and the bet
Problems
P1, P2
Insight
Up to 1 in 5 recent negative reviews of the ET app is about ads or upsells after paying. Renewal is where subscription money is decided, and today the weeks before renewal are when a member is most likely to leave.
The bet
Members stop being sold to, see what they got, and can pause instead of leaving.
Hypothesis
If members are never sold to and can see the value they got, renewal rises by at least two points.
2
BuildWhat the first version holds, who builds it, and when
First version
  • One set of rules for who sees what, read by the website, both apps and the sales dialer (F1, F2)
  • "I paid, let me in" in one tap, and self-serve invoices and cancelling (F3, F4)
  • A shared member status that every system reads, so the next three pieces have something to build on
Not yet
  • The value receipt (F14), Investor Wrapped, and pause beside cancel are built in months 4 to 6, for the renewal test
Team
A product manager, three engineers across web and apps, the sales operations lead for dialer rules, a data analyst, and finance for renewal measurement.
When
Days 1 to 60 for F1 to F4, as the plan sets out (F1 in days 1 to 30, F2 to F4 in days 31 to 60); months 4 to 6 for the receipt, Wrapped and pause.
Depends on
One member status that every system reads; pre-debit notices under the RBI's e-mandate rules; billing support for a pause.
3
LaunchHow it reaches users, and the line that stops it
How
The plan ships F1 and F2 to every member in the first 60 days, so the test on the Ideas page measures what the rest adds on top: the value receipt, Investor Wrapped and pause go to half of the members renewing over two quarters, months 7 to 12; the other half keep today's renewal experience.
Rollout
  1. 1F1 and F2 for all members, days 1 to 60
  2. 2Receipt, Wrapped and pause for half of renewing members, months 7 to 12
  3. 3All members, if the gate is passed

Stop if: renewal in the half that got it is not at least two points higher than in the other half, over two quarters.

4
MarketWho it is for, what we say, and where
Position
Members are never sold to. They see what their membership did for them.
For
Members within 60 days of renewal, then lapsed members.
Message
"Here is what your membership did for you this year."
Channels
  • The value receipt on the 1st of each month and seven days before renewal
  • Investor Wrapped, made to share, which carries ET into members' own networks
  • The renewal page itself
Moment
The year-end recap season, when Investor Wrapped goes out.
Price
No change to the price. Pause is free.
5
ScalePhases, each with a gate to pass first
  1. Months 13 to 18Receipt, Wrapped and pause for all members, if the gate is passedGate: Renewal at least two points up; the aim is five points, up to about ₹2.4 crore a year on public numbers
  2. Every year afterInvestor Wrapped each year; win-back offers for lapsed membersGate: Ad revenue per non-member session holds, as the plan's guardrail
Run it
Weekly report on paywalls shown to members (target: zero) and billing tickets per 1,000 members. Recaps leave out holdings unless the member chooses to share them.
6
UpgradesWhat comes after, from ideas already scored
Version 2
  • Gift a month: each member can give a colleague a month of Prime (I10)
  • Day passes for big days, paid by UPI, as a way back for lapsed members (I17)
Version 3
  • ET Prime for Teams: company seats with sector briefs (I9)
  • Callback-only selling, with every call scored for intent and conduct (I13)

Big bet 3

Ask ET: an answer engine, and an archive that earns

Built on: Big bet 3 on the Ideas page · The plan · The fixes

1
IdeaThe problem, the insight and the bet
Problems
P4, P6
Insight
Google search referrals to publishers fell 40.2% in a year, while weekly use of AI chatbots for news rose from 7% to 10% in the countries surveyed. Readers increasingly ask rather than search.
The bet
Answers drawn only from ET's reporting, with links, and the archive licensed to AI companies.
Hypothesis
If readers can ask ET and get cited, dated answers, more of them sign in and start Prime, and ET depends less on Google.
2
BuildWhat the first version holds, who builds it, and when
First version
  • Answers drawn only from ET's own stories, each citing the stories it used, with their dates
  • "I do not know" when the archive is silent
  • Three free answers a day; members get unlimited answers
  • A weekly sample of answers checked by editors
Not yet
  • WhatsApp answers (I14) and archive licensing (I6) wait for the second and third releases
Team
A product manager, two search engineers, a web engineer, an editor who owns answer quality, and a legal reviewer for rights.
When
Months 4 to 6, after the first 90 days, so the newsroom and legal reviews start with My Portfolio settled.
Depends on
A rights check first, since wire, syndicated and partner content may not be ET's to use; a full index of the archive; a cost per answer that the sign-ins can carry.
3
LaunchHow it reaches users, and the line that stops it
How
Ask ET on 10% of article pages for six weeks. Measure sign-ins, answer ratings and Prime starts from answers.
Rollout
  1. 110% of article pages, six weeks
  2. 2All article pages, if the gate is passed
  3. 3A search box on the ET home page

Stop if: fewer than 2% of answer users sign in, or if editors checking a weekly sample find more than 1 answer in 50 wrong.

4
MarketWho it is for, what we say, and where
Position
Answers on Indian business you can check: every claim cited and dated.
For
Readers who arrive with a question, from search and social, and members who want the depth behind an answer.
Message
"Ask ET. Every answer shows its sources."
Channels
  • An "Ask ET about this" box on article pages
  • Stories on big days that invite questions
  • Later, WhatsApp (I14)
Moment
Budget day, when questions about what a change means for a reader peak.
Price
Three free answers a day; unlimited for members, with the Prime pieces behind them.
5
ScalePhases, each with a gate to pass first
  1. Months 7 to 9Every article page, then a search box on the home pageGate: Sign-in rate above the stop line; wrong answers below 1 in 50
  2. Months 10 to 18WhatsApp (I14), then archive licensing (I6), only for content ET ownsGate: A rights ledger signed off by legal before any deal
Run it
Cost per answer, the editors' weekly sample, and a rights ledger for every source used.
6
UpgradesWhat comes after, from ideas already scored
Version 2
  • Answers inside My Portfolio, about a reader's own stocks, joining big bets 1 and 3
  • Market-hours answers on live prices, as part of market-hours mode (I7)
Version 3
  • ET Prime for Teams, with Ask ET beside the sector briefs (I9)
  • Callback-only selling that starts from what a reader asked (I13)
