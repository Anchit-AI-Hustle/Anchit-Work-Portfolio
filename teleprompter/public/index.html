Prompter Cam

Read your script. Look at the lens. Nail the take.

A free teleprompter that sits right under your camera, with virtual backgrounds and one-click recording. Your scripts and settings are saved to your account, so they are waiting on any device.

  • Unlimited scripts, each with its own speed, size and look
  • Blur, colour, studio or your own image as background
  • Record 16:9, 9:16 reels or square, up to 4K
  • Video never leaves your device
