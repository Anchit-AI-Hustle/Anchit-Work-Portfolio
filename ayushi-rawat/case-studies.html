Case 05 · Strategy caseCRM & lifecycle · real estate

Lead to site visit: a CRM lifecycle for real estate

Strategy case · how she would build it

This case joins her two careers: property marketing at SPN Propservices and Salesforce at VOIS. In property, enquiries often go cold between the form fill and the site visit. Marketing reports leads, sales reports visits, and nobody owns the gap. This is her plan to own it.

NoteThis is a strategy case, not a past result. The stages and metrics show how she would set up and measure the programme. No outcome figures are claimed.

The journey, stage by stage

01Capture

Every source (portals, social, events, walk-ins) goes into one CRM, with the source tagged and duplicates merged.

Metric · cost per lead by source
02Qualify

Leads scored on budget, location, timeline and intent. Hot leads go to sales with a response-time target.

Metric · speed to lead
03Nurture

Journeys by buyer stage: launch updates, project comparisons and financing explainers, by email and opted-in WhatsApp.

Metric · re-engagement rate
04Visit

Easy booking, reminders before the visit, and a follow-up within 24 hours after it.

Metric · lead → visit rate
05Close

Clear deal stages in the CRM, with collateral matched to the objections sales hears most.

Metric · visit → booking rate
06Refer

Onboarding after booking, then a referral ask while the buyer is happiest.

Metric · referral share of bookings

Closing the loop

One dashboard joins each lead’s source to its booking, so budget moves to the channels that produce bookings, not just leads. It is the same reporting discipline behind her 50+ dashboards at VOIS.

Guardrails

  • Consent first: WhatsApp opt-in under Meta’s business policy, and TRAI rules (DND preferences, DLT-registered templates) for SMS and calls
  • Personal data handled in line with India’s DPDP Act, 2023
  • One record per buyer, with required fields enforced
  • A weekly review of the response-time target with the sales lead

Built on

  • Campaign and collateral work at SPN Propservices (Case 01)
  • Salesforce automation and dashboards at VOIS (Case 02)
  • Training and adoption at VOIS (Case 03)

What it shows

She would not stop at the lead. She would follow each buyer to the booking and hold marketing to it.

Case 06 · Strategy caseOperating plan

The first 90 days as CRM & Lifecycle Marketing Manager

Strategy case · how she would run the role

A hiring manager wants to know what happens after the offer letter. This is her plan: audit what exists, fix what leaks, then grow what works, with the reason she can deliver each phase.

NoteThis is an operating plan, not a past result. Each phase lists the experience it rests on.

Days 1–30

Audit

  • Data health: duplicates, missing fields, untagged sources
  • Every live journey and automation listed, with broken ones fixed first
  • One funnel dashboard everyone agrees to use
  • A one-to-one with each stakeholder in sales, product and support

Why she can: 2,000+ tickets resolved and 50+ dashboards built. She knows where CRMs break.

Days 31–60

Build

  • A lifecycle map: welcome, activation, re-engagement, win-back
  • Segments by behaviour and stage, not only demographics
  • A testing plan that changes one variable per test
  • A written lead-handover agreement between marketing and sales

Why she can: she configured Sales and Service Cloud automation and supported 10+ campaigns.

Days 61–90

Scale

  • Holdout groups to prove each journey’s real, incremental lift
  • A monthly business review on pipeline and retention
  • Playbooks written down so the work outlasts the person
  • Team training on the new journeys and dashboards

Why she can: she trained 200+ people, cut onboarding time 20% and was part of 25% efficiency gains.

The scorecard she would report

  • Qualified pipeline created
  • Conversion rate at each funnel stage
  • Retention and repeat rate
  • Revenue influenced by lifecycle programmes

What it shows

She would arrive with a plan, report on the numbers that matter, and leave systems that keep running.
