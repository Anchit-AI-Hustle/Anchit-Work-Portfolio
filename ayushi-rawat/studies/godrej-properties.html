Brand case study 03 · Real estate · Lead-to-booking CRM

More homes to sell. Each enquiry matters more.

Across India’s top seven cities, new launches grew 18% in July–September 2026 while sales grew 3%.67 Buyers have more projects to compare, and developers have more inventory to sell. This study is Ayushi’s CRM journey from first enquiry to booking for Godrej Properties in Delhi NCR. It brings together her property marketing at SPN Propservices and her Salesforce work at VOIS.

The short version

The situation

A record year, a crowded market

Godrej Properties booked a record ₹34,171 Cr in FY26 and topped Q1 FY27 bookings among 28 listed developers, while their combined bookings fell 21%.14

The gap

After the sale is measured; before it is not public

The company reports a 71% NPS, from half-yearly surveys and surveys at four moments in the customer lifecycle.5 How enquiries reach a site visit is not published.

The play

Own the gap between form and visit

Speed to lead, a site-visit journey that removes the reasons people don’t turn up, and nurture that answers what buyers say matters most.

What Godrej Properties runs today, and the market around it

Where the value leaks

The gap between the form and the visit

01 · Speed

The first useful reply may win the visit

With unsold inventory up 12% across the top seven cities, buyers have more projects to compare. Ayushi’s hypothesis is that the first useful response wins a disproportionate share of visits. Her risk table tests it first.

Evidence: fact 6 for the market; the hypothesis is hers.

02 · No-shows

A booked visit is not a visit

Traffic, family schedules and second thoughts empty the sales gallery. Reminders with the route, the RM’s name and the unit they asked about lower the effort of turning up.

Godrej already surveys customers at a “site visit” moment (fact 5). Whether that covers pre-booking visits needs checking; if not, the same survey can be extended to them.

03 · Affordability

NCR prices rose fastest

Affordability is a top-three factor and NCR prices rose most. Nurture that explains payment plans and total cost answers the question buyers bring to the visit.

Evidence: facts 6 and 7.

04 · Attribution

Marketing counts leads; sales counts bookings

Unless each booking is traced back to its source, budget follows lead volume, not bookings. Closing that loop is a reporting job.

From Ayushi’s work at SPN Propservices, where her campaigns contributed to a 20% rise in qualified property leads.

How might we

…get more NCR enquiries to a site visit, and more visits to a booking, while the buyer is still comparing several projects?

The journey

Enquiry to booking, stage by stage

StageWhat happensOwnerMetric
1 · CaptureEvery source (portals, ads, channel partners, walk-ins) lands in one CRM record, tagged by source, with duplicates merged.Marketing opsCost per enquiry, by source
2 · RespondAn instant acknowledgement, then a call from a named RM inside an agreed time limit.Pre-salesTime to first call
3 · QualifyBudget, location, configuration, timeline and loan need are captured as required fields, which gives a simple fit score.Pre-salesQualified share of enquiries
4 · VisitBooking, a reminder the day before and two hours before, with the map pin and RM name.Pre-sales + CRMVisit show rate
5 · Follow upWithin 24 hours: the floor plan they saw, a payment plan, and an answer to the objection they raised.SalesVisit → booking rate
6 · NurtureNot ready yet: monthly construction updates and launch news for the project they asked about.CRMRe-engagement rate

The plan

Three plays, each with a trigger, a message and a test

Play 1 · Speed to lead

The first useful reply may win

Acknowledge every enquiry at once with something useful, the brochure and price range for the project asked about, then route it to a named RM with a call-back clock that a manager can see.

Trigger
Any new enquiry, from any source.
Channels
WhatsApp for buyers who opted in on the form. For the rest, one service SMS about their own enquiry on a DLT-registered template, then a call. Any promotional SMS only with recorded consent.
Routing
By project and language. Unanswered enquiries move to the next RM automatically.
Visible to
A daily dashboard of time to first call, by RM and source.
Sample copy · written for this study
WhatsApp · opted ininstantThanks for your interest in our Gurugram projectBrochure and price range attached. Ritu, your relationship manager, will call you shortly.
SMS · DLT templateinstantThank you for your enquiry. Your RM Ritu will call you shortly. Brochure: [link]
Play 2 · Visit show rate

Make turning up the easy option

Once a visit is booked, every message removes a reason not to come: where to park, who to ask for, how long it takes, and the unit they asked about kept ready to see.

Trigger
Visit booked. Reminders at T−1 day and T−2 hours. Reschedule link in each one.
After
Within 24 hours: floor plan, payment plan, and an answer to their main objection, logged by the RM.
Feedback
A one-question survey after each pre-booking visit, modelled on the existing customer NPS, joined to the lead record so a low score triggers a manager call.
Stop rule
No-shows get one reschedule offer, then move to nurture.
Sample copy · written for this study
WhatsApp · T−1 day6:00 pmSee you tomorrow at 11 amRitu will meet you at the sales gallery. Parking is at Gate 2. 📍 Map pin
WhatsApp · +24 hoursnext dayThe 3 BHK you liked, on paperFloor plan and a construction-linked payment plan attached, as discussed.
Play 3 · Nurture and trust

Answer what buyers say matters most

NCR buyers rank location, builder reputation and affordability highest. Nurture content follows the same order, for buyers who are not ready to book yet, and shows construction progress, since most NCR buyers prefer homes still being built or at launch.

Trigger
Qualified, no visit in 14 days, or visited with no booking in 30 days.
Content
Location: connectivity and schools nearby. Reputation: construction updates. Affordability: payment plans.
Cadence
Fortnightly, then monthly, by email, and by WhatsApp or SMS only for buyers who opted in. Any reply or click goes straight back to the RM.
Stop rule
Opt-out at any step. Archive after 6 months without engagement.
Sample copy · written for this study
Email · subjectweek 2Your commute from here, mappedDrive times to Cyber City and the airport, at 9 am and 6 pm.
Email · subjectmonth 2Tower B reached its 12th floor this monthPhotos from the site, and dates for the next open house.

How she would prove it

One dashboard, from source to booking

North-star metricBookings per 100 qualified enquiries, by sourceBudget moves to the sources that produce bookings, not just leads.
Stage metrics

Primary

  • Time to first call
  • Visit show rate
  • Visit → booking rate
Guardrails

Must not get worse

  • Site-visit NPS
  • Opt-outs and DND complaints
  • Cost per booking
Test design

Decision rule

  • Pilot on one NCR project, with a comparable project as control
  • Or alternate weeks, if one project is all there is
  • Scale only if show rate rises and NPS holds

What could be wrong

Riskiest assumptions, cheapest tests

AssumptionWhy it might failCheapest test
Speed matters as much in property as elsewhereHome buyers decide over weeks, so an hour may not matter.Compare visit rates for enquiries called within 1 hour vs later, from existing data.
No-shows are an effort problemThey may be a price or interest problem instead.Ask no-shows one question on reschedule: “What got in the way?”
Channel partners will share dataBrokers may hold back buyer details to protect their commission.Start with direct enquiries only; add partner leads once the process works.
RMs will log follow-upsLogging feels like admin, and fields get skipped.Make three fields required and show RMs their own conversion, not just the team’s.

Consent first: WhatsApp only with opt-in under Meta’s business policy, and TRAI rules (DND preferences, DLT-registered templates) for SMS and calls. Personal data is handled in line with India’s DPDP Act, 2023.
