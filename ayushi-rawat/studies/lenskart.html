Case studies/Lenskart

Brand case study 06 · Omnichannel eyewear · Membership & replenishment

Half of India’s eye tests are first ones. Make each one a relationship.

Lenskart ran 0.6 crore eye tests in India in one quarter, about half of them someone’s first. In its own words, “eye tests create new customers, Gold converts them into recurring relationships.” This study is Ayushi’s lifecycle plan for that handover: from a first eye test, to a first pair, to a Gold membership that renews.

The short version

The situation

A repeat engine already works

Customer accounts acquired in FY23 bought 3.6 pairs of eyeglasses in two years, against an India average of 1.8. Gold has 93.5 lakh active members.31

The gap

Newcomers and the 365-day clock

First-time eye-test customers are new to glasses, and every Gold membership comes up for renewal after a year.

The play

Care, value, and the refill

Aftercare for a first pair, a Gold value tracker before renewal, and contact-lens refills timed to the pack, with an annual eye-test recall.

What Lenskart runs today

Where the value leaks

Four handovers a lifecycle team owns

01 · New to glasses

A first exam is a first habit

Someone wearing glasses for the first time has questions: headaches, adjustment, care. Answering them early is what turns one purchase into a relationship.

Evidence: fact 3.

02 · The Gold clock

Renewal is decided by the year before it

A membership used once and forgotten is hard to renew. Benefits that refresh twice a month give a reason to come back that most memberships lack.

Evidence: facts 4 and 5. Renewal rates are not public; this is Ayushi’s hypothesis.

03 · Lenses run out

Replenishment on a rhythm

Contact lenses repeat by nature. A reminder timed to the pack, with next-day delivery, keeps the refill from going to the chemist next door.

Evidence: the contact-lens note above.

04 · Eyes change

The next exam is the next pair

Prescriptions change. An eye-test reminder is a service, and an eye test is where Lenskart already finds its customers.

Lenskart publishes no recall cadence; the interval is to be tested, not assumed.

How might we

…make a first eye test the start of a relationship that renews every year, across the store and the app?

The plan

Three plays, each with a trigger, a message and a test

Play 1 · First-pair aftercare

Help them get used to it

For customers whose first-ever eye test ended in a first pair, a short aftercare sequence: what adjustment feels like, how to clean the lenses, where to get a free fit check. The second pair comes up only once the first is working.

Trigger
First-time eye exam followed by a first purchase, online or in store.
Sequence
Day 2: adjustment tips. Day 10: “How are your new glasses?” with a store fit check. Day 30: a second pair (sunglasses or screen glasses), shown as a Gold benefit if they are a member.
Channels
SMS and email, which Lenskart’s policy names; app notifications for app users.
Stop rule
A poor reply on day 10 goes to the store for a fix, not to a sale.
Sample copy · written for this study
SMSday 2New glasses can feel strange for a few days. That’s normal. 3 tips to settle in: [link]
Email · subjectday 10How are your new glasses?If anything feels off, visit any store for a fit check.
Play 2 · Gold, made visible

A membership you can see working

Show each member what Gold has done for them and what is waiting this month: benefits used, money saved, family members sharing it. Thirty days before the year ends, the renewal message is that record, not a price.

Trigger
Monthly benefit refresh; renewal at day 335 of 365.
Message
“Your Gold this year”: pairs bought, savings, the benefit available now.
Segments
Members who have not used a benefit in 90 days get a store or home try-on invitation instead.
Stop rule
One Gold message a month; renewal reminders stop on renewal.
Sample copy · written for this study
App cardday 335Your Gold year: 3 pairs, ₹2,400 savedRenew before 14 Nov and keep this month’s benefit.
SMSmonthlyYour Gold benefit is ready this month. Use it in any store or on the app.
Play 3 · Refill and recall

The refill before they run out, the exam before it’s overdue

Contact-lens buyers get a refill reminder timed to their pack size, with next-day delivery where it runs. All customers get an eye-test reminder after a tested interval, offered at the nearest store or at home.

Trigger
Days since the lens order reach the pack’s wear period minus 7; months since the last eye test reach the recall interval under test.
Offer
Gold’s extra 10% on Aqualens and Aquacolor contacts for members; none for others at first.
Channels
SMS, email and app, to customers who have given marketing consent; prescription data used for reminders only with consent for that purpose.
Stop rule
One reminder per refill; the eye-test reminder at most twice a year.
Sample copy · written for this study
SMSday 23 of 30Your monthly lenses run out next week. Reorder now; next-day delivery where available: [link]
Email · subjectmonth 12A year since your last eye testBook an eye test at a store near you, or at home.

How she would prove it

Measured against a holdout, not a hunch

North-star metricSecond purchases within 12 months, per 100 first-time eye-test customersThe handover from “first exam” to “customer for years”.
Per play

Primary

  • Second purchase within 90 days (Play 1)
  • Gold renewal rate vs holdout (Play 2)
  • Lens refills on time; tests booked from recall (Play 3)
Guardrails

Must not get worse

  • NPS on first-time customers
  • SMS opt-outs and DND complaints
  • Store fit-check load per store
Test design

Decision rule

  • A random 10% holdout per journey
  • Recall interval tested in two arms
  • Ship only if the lift beats the holdout and NPS holds

Rollout

Ninety days, store and app together

Days 1–30

Measure

  • Second-purchase rate for first-time exam customers
  • Gold benefit use and renewal, by tenure
  • Lens reorder intervals by pack size
Days 31–60

Launch Plays 1 and 3

  • Aftercare in two regions, with store teams briefed
  • Lens refills for monthly packs first
  • Weekly read-out against the holdout
Days 61–90

Add Play 2

  • Gold value tracker for members in months 9–11
  • Recall interval arms live
  • Playbook and training for store staff

What could be wrong

Riskiest assumptions, cheapest tests

AssumptionWhy it might failCheapest test
Aftercare earns a second purchaseMost people may not want a second pair for a long time.Read 90-day second purchase for aftercare vs. no message.
Seeing savings drives renewalLight users may have saved little.Show the tracker only above a savings threshold; test the threshold.
Lens refills can be timedPeople wear lenses part-time, so packs last longer.Learn each buyer’s real interval from their second order before reminding.
Stores can absorb more fit checksBusy stores may struggle at peak hours.Pilot in two regions and track fit-check load per store.

Consent first. Lenskart’s privacy policy says customers authorise contact by email, phone and SMS, including a 365-day window to call customers registered under DND.5 This plan does not rely on that window: it messages only customers who have given marketing consent, uses eye-test and prescription data for reminders only with consent for that purpose, follows TRAI rules for SMS, and handles personal data in line with India’s DPDP Act, 2023.
