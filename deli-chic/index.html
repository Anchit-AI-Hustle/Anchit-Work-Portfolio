ETHICS & STANDARDS

Sourced with conscience, not compromise.

Deli Chic was born from a fundamental standard: what we feed our families must be clean, transparent, and respectful of the animal. Most commercial poultry in NCR relies on preventive antibiotic treatments and artificial brine weight injection.

We source exclusively from audited, biosecure farms where birds are raised in open-air shelters on 100% vegetarian grain feed. Independent laboratory assays verify zero antibiotic residues, zero growth hormones, and zero synthetic tenderizers.

In our temperature-controlled Delhi central kitchen, each cut is prepared by trained seam butchers, immediately vacuum-sealed at peak freshness, and chilled to 0°C–4°C without ever touching frozen ice crystallization.

CRYOGENIC LOGISTICS

The cold-chain guarantee.

Freezing destroys meat at a cellular level, creating ice crystals that rupture fibers and leech natural juices upon thaw. Deli Chic cuts are never frozen. They exist in a strict, continuous 0°C–4°C refrigeration envelope from preparation to doorstep.

Cold-Chain Protocol:
• Nitrogen-flushed food grade barrier pouches
• Insulated multi-ply foil thermal delivery cases
• Non-toxic gel ice refrigerant maintain < 4°C for 8 hours
01

Audited Biosecure Farms

Healthy poultry raised in natural ventilated sheds, free from preventive antibiotics and growth hormones.

02

4°C Air-Chilled Preparation

Seam butchery performed in sanitized, climate-controlled cutting rooms with zero water bath absorption.

03

Hermetic Vacuum Packaging

Oxygen extraction locks in natural myoglobin and prevents bacterial growth without chemical preservatives.

04

Direct NCR Chilled Dispatch

Dispatched from your nearest local delivery hub in specialized temperature-monitored refrigerated boxes.
