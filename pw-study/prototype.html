    Weekly Progress Report on WhatsApp

    The PW Parent App already shows attendance, lecture, DPP and test stats, but a parent has to open it. The Weekly Progress Report pushes a plain-language summary on WhatsApp and links into the app. Who receives it depends on the learner's age. It is built from the same learner and signals as the Student app tab: drag the signals there and this message changes.

    Guardrails built in

    • The learner always sees exactly what is shared, and with whom.
    • One report a week, max one alert in between. No fear or rank-shaming language.
    • High-risk learners still behind after the 7-day plan get a human mentor call, never only a bot.
    • Offers appear only at natural moments (results, batch end), never inside a risk alert.
    • Under 18: offers and prices go only to the parent; the child sees an academic next step (DPDP Act 2023, s.9(3)).

    Renewals Dashboard

    The screen PW's retention team would open every Monday: who is drifting, what action each group gets, and what that is worth. Uses a made-up cohort of 5,000 paid learners so it runs without PW data.

    1Who is drifting

    Every paid learner gets a weekly risk score from their study signals (lecture pace, tests, inactivity, doubts, streak).

    2What we do about it

    Learners are grouped by the action they get. Click any row to see who is in it and how the ₹ is worked out.

    3What it is worth for PW

    A what-if calculator on PW's real FY26 numbers. Change any assumption and the total updates.

    1 · Who is drifting this week

    Share of the cohort in each risk tier. High = 60+ risk score, Medium = 35-59, Low = under 35.

    2 · What we do about it

    Sorted by expected extra fees, highest first. Click a column header to re-sort, or a row for detail.

    How to prove it (first 90 days)

    1
    Backtest, then pick 2 grades

    Go only if week-6 risk predicts non-renewal (AUC 0.70+). Pilot Class 10 and Class 11: both have a next batch.

    2
    90/10 holdout

    10% keep today's experience. About 13,300 learners detect +4pp on a 40% base at 80% power.

    3
    Primary metric

    Next-cycle enrolment within 30 days of batch end or results day.

    4
    Guardrails

    Notification opt-outs, refund rate, app rating, parent complaints.

    PW brand context schema

    Extracted from computed styles on pw.live (homepage, NEET Class 12 listing, a batch detail page) on 25 Oct 2026 with a headless browser. This prototype uses only these tokens. The PW logo is deliberately not reproduced.

    Colour tokens

    Type

    • Reddit Sans only, weights 400/500/600/700 (Noto Sans Devanagari added here for Hindi).
    • h1 40/50 700 · h2 32/48 700 · card title 20 600 · body 16/24 400 · label 12 500.
    • Buttons 16px: 400 in header, 600 for primary actions.

    Shape and depth

    • Buttons 6px radius, 48-56px tall. Tags 4px. Panels 8-12px. Batch cards 16px with 1px #D9DCE1 border, no shadow. Pills 9999px.
    • Shadows: light 0 1px 8px #00000014; detail cards 0 4px 16px rgba(27,33,36,.06).
    • Hero gradient: 270deg #EEF9FF → #F9EEFF → #F8F8FF → #F1F5FF.

    Batch card anatomy

    1. Banner with faculty art and plan strip ("Infinity, Pro").
    2. Orange category label (e.g. "Dropper") + language pill ("HINGLISH").
    3. Title 20/600, audience line, start and end dates.
    4. Price 18/700, struck MRP, green "% OFF"; dark "Buy Now" + outline chevron.

    Voice

    • Warm, aspirational, numbers-led ("Join 15 Million students", "24*7 Doubt Support").
    • English UI; Hindi carries the brand in names: Arjuna, Lakshya, Yakeen, Vidyapeeth, Pathshala.
    • Teachers are "Sir"; batches are labelled HINGLISH; emoji rare.
    • This concept keeps plain English feature names and UI, with Hinglish only inside student chat.
