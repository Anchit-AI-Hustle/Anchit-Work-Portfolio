In 60 seconds

The short version

1 · Two engines

Half of FY26 revenue is online at ₹4,104 per learner a year; offline earns ₹36,625 per student, about 9x more.

2 · Strong AI, aimed at sale one

Lead routing lifts revenue per lead ~10%; voice bots cut counselling cost ~75%; Ask AI and AI Guru answer doubts at scale.

3 · Retention tools exist, but scattered

Tracker 360, Prep Meter, the Parent App and 1:1 mentorship are live, but split across plans and apps, and mostly pull-based.

4 · The bet

Agla Kadam: one layer that reads those signals for every learner, acts by risk and age, and personalises the next batch. My estimate: about ₹85.3 cr ($8.97M) a year.

05 · Shortcomings

Five places the model leaks revenue

ShortcomingEvidenceSo what
1. Repeat business is not visibleNo renewal or repeat-purchase rate is disclosed in the FY26 letter or on the Q1 FY27 call; retention is only mentioned in passing. Fees are paid upfront, so drift in month three still counts as revenue.Drop-off hides until next year's non-renewal.
2. Retention tools are scattered and pull-basedTracker 360 in some plans, Prep Meter in Infinity, 1:1 mentorship in Infinity Pro, parent data in a separate app that has to be opened.The base-plan learners who drift most get the least help.
3. AI targets the first saleLead routing +10% revenue per lead; voice bots −75% counselling cost. No disclosed AI aimed at retention or renewal.AI wins the first sale; nothing aims at the second.
4. Revenue is calendar-bound79% of FY26 collections came March to September; Vishwas Diwas took ₹205 cr in 3 weeks; a NEET shift moved 1-1.5 lakh enrolments out of Q1.Peaks are mass events; next steps are not yet personal.
5. Offline growth must come from existing centresOffline ARPU −9% on mix; expansion now "opportunistic"; margin rests on ARPU and seat utilisation (1.77 → 1.99).PW's best offline leads are its own online learners.

07 · The bet

Agla Kadam ("next step"): a continuity layer on top of what PW already runs

No new learning content, faculty or capex. It connects the signals PW already collects to actions PW already knows how to take, for every learner on every plan.

01 · PULSE

Spot drift early

A weekly drop-off risk score for every paid learner, read from Tracker 360 and Prep Meter progress, lecture pace, tests, inactivity and doubts. Every score shows its reasons.

02 · RESCUE

Act by risk and age

High risk: human mentor call and a 7-day plan. Medium: AI Mentor catch-up. Plus a Weekly Pulse on WhatsApp that goes to the parent for under-18s and to the learner for adults, linking into the Parent App.

03 · NEXT STEP

Personalise results day

A personal year report and the right next batch, plan upgrade or Vidyapeeth trial, timed to results and Vishwas Diwas. CuriousJr's next-year preview, extended to the main app. Offers only when on track.

Reuses (already live)

  • Tracker 360 and Prep Meter progress signals
  • AI Mentor, Ask AI and 1:1 mentors as the rescue channels
  • PW Parent App as the place a parent lands
  • Infinity and Infinity Pro plans; 353 centres
  • Lead AI and voice bots for follow-up calls

Adds (not found today)

  • One cross-plan drift score with reasons
  • Risk- and age-based routing of actions
  • Weekly Pulse pushed on WhatsApp
  • Results-day personal next step in the main app
  • Online-to-Vidyapeeth trial class for strong learners in centre cities
  • A holdout to prove incremental lift

10 · First 90 days

Prove lift in two categories before scaling

Weeks 1-3 · Baseline
  • Pull two past cohorts
  • Define drift and renewal
  • Backtest: does week-6 risk predict non-renewal?
Weeks 4-8 · Pilot
  • Class 11 JEE and NEET dropper
  • Pulse and Rescue live with a 90/10 holdout
  • Weekly Pulse for opted-in parents and learners
Weeks 9-13 · Next step
  • Batch-end report and next-batch card
  • Vidyapeeth trial in 5 centre cities
  • Read-out and scale decision
North star

Next-cycle enrolment within 30 days of batch end or results.

Leading

Lecture pace, 7-day reactivation, upgrade rate, trial-to-seat rate.

Guardrails

Opt-outs, refund rate, app rating, parent complaints.
