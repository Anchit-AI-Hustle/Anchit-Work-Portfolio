Growth, monetisation and AI architecture

FreeUseful shortlist and reminders; earn trust and activate.
PremiumExpert review, multi-round guidance and parent-ready decision summary.
InstitutionsOpt-in, qualified applicant demand; feedback on application status.
RetrieveRules, cutoff years, categories, quotas, deadlines and verified institution data.
RecommendDeterministic eligibility and evidence-backed explanations; abstain if uncertain.
ReviewHuman escalation for high-stakes choices; correction queue and consent controls.

Lifecycle: acquisition via search and exam content → consented profile → saved shortlist → relevant deadline reminders → expert consultation → application outcome. Trigger on meaningful events rather than blanket promotions. Parent sharing must be student-controlled. Institutional sponsorship must not alter organic fit.

SEO/AEO: use clear source dates, structured eligible schema, editorial authorship, FAQ answer quality, canonical URLs, language accessibility, internal links and exam-specific next actions. Audit Search Console, index coverage, Core Web Vitals and AI citation visibility before claiming defects.

Measurement and experimental design

MetricDefinition / decision
North starDistinct weekly students with a meaningful decision milestone: saved verified shortlist, completed expert action or confirmed application; deduplicate student/week.
ActivationNew eligible users saving a shortlist or plan / eligible new users.
Next-step rateStudents completing recommended step within 7 days / students shown a next step.
Counselling productivityQA-passed resolved cases / counsellor hours; appointment show rate.
MonetisationIncremental contribution = paid receipts + verified partner revenue − refunds − variable service, AI and messaging costs.
Trust guardrailsIncorrect guidance, calibration by cohort, complaint and refund rates, consent opt-outs and source freshness.

Experiment: randomise eligible engineering users to existing experience or saved plan + explanation. Primary metric: 7-day next-step completion. Proposed minimum decision threshold: +10% relative uplift (illustrative; power from baseline), no significant deterioration in trust and positive incremental contribution. Use concurrent control, pre-register sample size, monitor seasonality and avoid early peeking.

Illustrative inputs only, not company data.

90-day execution roadmap

Days 1–30Map data + funnels; 20–30 student/parent interviews; counsellor shadowing; baseline metrics.
Days 31–60Pilot Admissions Plan + Explainable Fit for one engineering cohort; controlled experiment.
Days 61–90Extend to counsellor workflow; assess incremental contribution and student outcomes; scale or iterate.

Scale gate: reliable source coverage, measurable progress lift, no trust regression and contribution economics that justify capacity. Prioritise engineering exam planning first, subject to internal data and calendar. Expand to NEET and other cohorts only after domain-specific validation.

Interview defence and leadership questions

Why not build a standalone AI chatbot?

Generic guidance is easy to replicate and can hallucinate. Careers360’s advantage is its structured data, ranking methodology and counsellor network. AI should explain and orchestrate a verifiable workflow, not invent probabilities.

How would you handle seasonality?

Use exam- and stage-specific cohorts, matched calendar windows, concurrent experiment controls and early-stage planning engagement. Extend to college readiness only after core outcomes are proven.

What if lead revenue conflicts with student fit?

Separate commercial placement from organic recommendations. Disclose sponsorship, require opt-in, audit ranking independence and measure accepted lead quality and complaints.

What would you ask leadership?

Which user cohort and revenue stream matter most today? Where is the largest verified funnel loss? What are paid package margins, counsellor capacity and data freshness SLAs? How are applications and admission outcomes verified?
