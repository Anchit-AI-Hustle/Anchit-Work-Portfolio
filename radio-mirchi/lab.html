Try the numbers: events, subscriptions and radio ads.

Event edition: profit after direct costs and break-even

All amounts are in rupees, excluding GST. Ticket price is net organiser receipt; no fees or taxes are deducted again. Variable cost is per paid attendee. Sponsorship and ancillary income must be contracted; allocated corporate overhead is excluded.

Formula: profit after direct costs = paid attendees × (net ticket receipt − variable cost) + sponsorship + ancillary − fixed costs. Break-even attendees = ceiling(max(0, fixed − sponsorship − ancillary) ÷ ticket profit after direct costs), subject to positive ticket profit after direct costs and capacity.

Subscriber group: first-year profit after direct costs

This model starts with paying users. It is not suitable for treating a complimentary hardware bundle as full-price revenue. Annual price below is net recorded service revenue per paid year, excluding taxes. Cost-share and retention inputs are hypothetical, not Gaana disclosures.

Formula: annual unit profit after direct costs before acquisition = annual net revenue × (1 − variable cost share) − annual service cost. First-year user group profit after direct costs subtracts acquisition spend. Renewal illustration applies the same revenue and costs for each customer to renewing users, excludes new acquisition and is not lifetime value.

Radio: percentage of ad time sold versus price

An illustrative period with a fixed pool of available ad seconds. Changing utilisation and price earned does not account for changes in station, time of day, inventory quality, collections or extra operating costs.

Decision rule

A revenue increase does not establish a profit increase. Use these models to expose assumptions, then add the actual cost, cash and customer constraints before recommending a decision.
