Assumptions & definitions

All models are hypothetical scenarios, not Gaana or ENIL operating disclosures. Default inputs and presets do not report actual CAC, churn, paid users, margins, ticket costs, radio yield or product impact. The observed ₹709/year public offer is not the same as the illustrative ₹600 of net recorded subscription service revenue in the first model.

Gaana subscriptions

Starts with already-paying users: annual unit contribution = net recorded annual service revenue × (1 − variable cost share) − annual direct service cost. Year-one cohort contribution = users × (annual unit contribution − acquisition cost). Renewals are a separate next-year illustration using the same unit margin and the assumed renewal rate. Paid renewal does not affect the upfront Year-one contribution. CAC recovery months spread annual unit contribution evenly as an illustrative comparison, not the cash collection schedule, customer lifetime value or statutory accounting. Recognised revenue must be net of applicable tax, refunds, store / partner shares and revenue recognition rules.

FM radio yield

Revenue = available ad seconds × sold percentage × net realised ₹ per sold second. The alternative discounts net price while varying sold seconds. The model does not subtract any incremental sales/production expenses, model differences by station/daypart, or imply that more ad loading is listener-safe. Profit needs a separate cost bridge.

Live events

Event revenue = paid attendees × net organiser receipt per ticket + contracted sponsorship + net ancillary income. Direct cost = fixed direct spend + attendees × variable cost. Ticket receipt is after platform fees and relevant taxes; GST, allocations and corporate overhead must be handled consistently and not subtracted twice. Sponsor income must be contracted and collectible; exclude speculative projections. Break-even attendees are the ceiling of uncovered fixed costs divided by net ticket contribution (when positive), capped by available ticket capacity. Attendance is paid admissions only, not gross footfall.

Decision integrity: validate all baseline numbers, cohort definitions, cash timing, GST, corporate overhead and net organiser receipt with finance before committing spend. Prefer controlled incrementality over a favorable illustrative outcome.
