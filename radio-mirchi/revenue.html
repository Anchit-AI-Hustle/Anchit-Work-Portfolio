The radio equation

Model

Net Revenue from paid radio ad time = ad seconds available × percentage of ad time sold × actual revenue per second. Use the same city, time of day and time period for every input.

A 5% price reduction needs more than 5% additional sold volume to keep revenue unchanged: 1 ÷ 0.95 − 1 = 5.26%. If capacity or listener ad tolerance is already constrained, the extra volume may not be achievable. Discounting should be evaluated against total profit after direct costs, advertiser retention and future pricing expectations.

Percentage of ad time sold and price are not independent. Prime time, local festivals, premium sponsorships and bundled contracts alter the inventory mix. A national average can hide both healthy cities and weak stations.

Questions that prevent misleading revenue stories

  • Is ticket revenue shown gross or net of platform fees, taxes and organiser share?
  • Is an event owned, co-produced, commissioned or only promoted? Branding alone does not establish IP ownership.
  • Do digital solutions include media purchased on behalf of clients? Separate billings from retained margin.
  • When is sponsorship cash collected versus recorded as revenue?
  • Does a bundle pay Gaana on shipment, activation or usage? What is the effective net consideration?
  • Are sold to existing customers revenues extra or shifted from another Mirchi budget?
