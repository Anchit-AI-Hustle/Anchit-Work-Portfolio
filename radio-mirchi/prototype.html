Independent business case · Radio Mirchi

Dealer campaign review

Illustrative Delhi showroom campaign. Compare delivery, customer response and the money Mirchi would retain from a renewal.

What the dealer bought

Objective: encourage weekend test drives. The example booking covers 30 radio ads and one host appearance. The station log confirms 28 ads and the appearance. Two ads remain due. Agree replacement slots with the dealer.

What the dealer can measure

The dealer recorded 18 test drives during the campaign weekend. Eight participants used the campaign booking code. Code use shows a recorded response. A comparable weekend or a controlled test would help estimate additional visits caused by the campaign.

All amounts and counts here are examples. The dealer sees delivery and response. The station uses the renewal calculation internally.
