A reliable structure for my examples

  • Situation: what customer or business problem existed and how it was established.
  • Ownership: the decision I owned and the people I worked with.
  • Action: what changed and why other options were rejected.
  • Evidence: time period, base number, result and how causality was assessed.
  • Learning: what I would repeat, change or investigate next.
