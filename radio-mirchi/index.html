The 60-second business brief

Radio Mirchi is the radio and entertainment brand operated by ENIL. A local station brings listeners to its hosts and shows. Advertisers buy airtime, sponsored segments and city campaigns to reach that audience. S7

ENIL also reports on businesses such as Gaana. Its group revenue and profit include activities beyond radio. For station decisions, look at the city, the show, ad sales and repeat bookings. S1

Three decisions I would put on the table

  • What makes a listener choose this show again tomorrow?
  • What would convince a local advertiser to book a second campaign?
  • Would another edition of a city event cover its costs and earn repeat sponsorship?
