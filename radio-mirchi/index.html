The 60-second business brief

Radio Mirchi is the flagship brand within listed Entertainment Network (India) Limited (ENIL), part of the Times Group. Think of it as a city-centric entertainment and advertiser business with several ways to earn: broadcast advertising, branded solutions, events, digital services and subscriptions. S7 S6

My thesis: the opportunity is to make Mirchi’s local trust, creators and cultural IP convert into repeat advertiser spend and durable consumer relationships. Revenue growth is useful only when contribution, working capital and customer value improve with it.

Three decisions I would put on the table

Core business

Protect radio’s value

Improve yield and the advertiser’s reason to renew. A discount that fills inventory can still destroy contribution. Build proof around a defined city outcome.

Events

Scale the right events

Choose repeatable formats with committed sponsorship, clear cash exposure and a realistic break-even ticket count. Evaluate each city and edition independently.

Digital

Make distribution pay back

Judge Gaana partnerships by activation, retained listening and renewal contribution. Devices shipped, trials started and social views are intermediate signals.
