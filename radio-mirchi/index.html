Independent business case · Radio Mirchi

How should Gaana win listening while Mirchi builds real relationships?

A product case for fixing Gaana music listening, keeping Mirchi distinct for RJs, communities and original shows, and proving repeat engagement and commercial value.

Explore the product decisions
FULL CONTEXT FIRST

Gaana can own music. Mirchi can own the relationships. Here is the case in 30 seconds.

Entertainment Network India Limited (ENIL) owns both Radio Mirchi (FM radio and live events) and Gaana (paid music streaming). Before diving into individual pages, here is the full business and product context.

1 · BUSINESS PRESSURE₹33 Cr digital revenue, ₹7.5 Cr EBITDA loss

In Q2 FY27, digital was 29% of domestic revenue, yet higher acquisition cost pushed Gaana’s breakeven target out by 1 to 2 quarters.

2 · MARKET SHIFTGaana usage fell 32% to 22% (2021 to 2024)

After moving to paid-only streaming in 2022, Gaana lost urban India share while Spotify rose from 24% to 42% (YouGov).

3 · PRODUCT REALITYPaywalls fire before trust is built

Observer-supplied iPhone screens show pricing math mismatches (₹58.27/mo on ₹709/yr) and mini-player CTA overlap; the recurring 1 to 2 minute popup is tester-reported and needs reproduction.

4 · CORE THESISKeep music and community jobs distinct

Fix Gaana's listening friction first. Test Mirchi as a separate host, community and original-content destination, with shared consent and clear links between products.

The recommendation in one sentence

Make the app easy and trustworthy first. Then test whether familiar Mirchi voices and Indian music moments give people a reason to listen again. See the steps.

The decision facing Mirchi Digital

When a listener hears a Mirchi host on FM or sees a clip online, can Mirchi help them find the full show, return next week and eventually pay for more? Start with one city and one recurring show. Mirchi supplies the host, programme and promotion. A Gaana product partner would own changes to its player, plans and billing.

In July–September 2026 ENIL reported ₹33 crore in digital revenue, up 4.6% year on year and equal to 29% of domestic revenue. Digital still recorded an EBITDA loss of ₹7.5 crore, compared with ₹9.8 crore a year earlier. EBITDA measures earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation. Management flagged a possible one- to two-quarter delay to Gaana’s breakeven aim because bringing in customers cost more. ENIL earnings call, November 2026

Who needs a better product?

  1. The listenerA city commuter knows the host’s voice but may not know the show title. A short link should open the episode the host just mentioned.
  2. The subscriberA listener arriving through earbuds or a trial needs a reason to return after the offer ends. A saved local show can give them one.
  3. The advertiserA local buyer wants to know whether people listened, enquired or visited. Each outcome needs its own count and cost.

ENIL reported 153.1 million monthly active users across its digital properties and around 20 million for Gaana. These have different scopes and potentially overlapping audiences. Dividing one by the other would give a misleading conversion rate. ENIL earnings call, November 2026

Why start with returning listeners?

Gaana moved to paid streaming in 2022 while rivals could still introduce people to music for free. In YouGov’s urban India survey, Gaana usage fell from 32% to 22% between 2021 and 2024, while Spotify rose from 24% to 42%. A familiar city host is one reason to choose Mirchi that a competing music catalogue cannot reproduce. See the four-service comparison

Which alternatives were considered?

A cheaper weekly pass may attract buyers who would have bought the monthly plan. Buying installs can increase acquisition cost. A local series can use existing distribution, but still costs money to produce and promote. Test repeat use first, then test a paid offer with listeners who have experienced the show.

The proposed listening journey

  1. DiscoverHear a short address on FM or tap a tagged link on a social clip.
  2. ListenOpen the exact episode in the app or a lightweight web player. Offer city and language selection only when useful.
  3. ReturnSave the show and resume it. Ask permission for a reminder when the next episode arrives.
  4. Choose a planExplain the price, billing period and renewal before purchase. Offer a relevant series or a longer ad-free session.

The parallel advertiser opportunity is to sell sponsorship for a defined audience with evidence of delivery. Obtain rights and confirm eligible placements before quoting. What M-Ping, Brewery and event sponsorship sell

How to run a controlled pilot

Weeks 1–2: the station producer selects a rights-cleared show. The digital team tests links on mobile web and installed apps. Analytics defines an eligible visitor and records playback, return and payment. Weeks 3–6: compare a direct episode landing page with the usual destination, using random assignment within each tagged source. Keep the same creative, programme and offer. Report FM and social separately. Weeks 7–10: follow each group for 30 days. Compare like-for-like listeners before extending the trial.

How much can the team spend?

Illustration: ₹30,000 in production and promotion divided by 300 additional paying listeners is ₹100 per new payer. At ₹25 a month left after variable costs per active payer, that takes four paid months to recover before churn. If many listeners cancel in month two, the campaign can lose money even with strong sign-ups. Explore the payback scenarios

When should Mirchi expand?

Primary decision: expand only if the test produces more returning listeners and positive additional contribution over the agreed payback window. Track first plays per eligible arrival, seven-day return per first-time listener, first paid renewal per eligible subscriber and contribution after rights, production, partner fees and promotion. Set the minimum useful lift and sample size with finance and analytics before launch.

Pause if playback failures, refund requests or complaints rise beyond the pre-agreed limits. A positive conversion result with weaker renewal calls for a longer observation window. If the listener trial succeeds, take a separately costed sponsorship package to a city advertiser. Explore an advertiser campaign review
