The plan

WindowWorkConcrete outputGate
Days 1-15Agree mandate; meet commercial, content, events, digital, finance and city teams. Observe customer and internal journeys.Business tree, metric definitions, platform inventory, baseline data-quality review.One accountable owner and reliable success measure per pilot.
Days 16-30Size the top two needs; validate existing work; agree counterfactual and cash exposure.Pilot briefs, baseline cohorts, tracking design, cost / benefit ranges, stop rules.Approve one consumer and one commercial pilot only if capacity permits.
Days 31-60Ship bounded changes; instrument entitlement and delivery; monitor service quality.Working activation journey or advertiser workflow; weekly economics review.Results are measurable and guardrails stable before more traffic or cities.
Days 61-90Read mature-enough cohorts; close sponsor/vendor cash loops; compare rollout groups.Decision memo: scale, iterate or stop; owner, budget and next-quarter plan.Scale by incremental contribution and customer value, not activity counts.

Annual Gaana renewal cannot be proved in 90 days. Use short-term activation and retention as leading indicators, maintain the cohort for later renewal measurement and avoid claiming validated lifetime value too early.

Operating cadence

Analysis

Weekly business review

A compact dashboard: commercial contribution and collections; consumer activation and retention; delivery exceptions; experiment status; key dependencies. Discuss decisions and surprises, not a catalogue of activities.

Analysis

Fortnightly learning review

Show one customer journey or workflow observation, test results and what changed in the hypothesis. Include negative evidence and experiments stopped.

Analysis

Monthly portfolio review

Compare investment and returns by business. Protect committed delivery, revisit capital exposure and stop pilots that are not earning the next tranche of effort.

Collaboration and decision rights

PartnerWhat to agree
FinanceRevenue definitions, contribution waterfall, cash timing and investment hurdle.
Sales & agenciesCustomer need, offer scope, pricing authority and renewal ownership.
Content / RJsEditorial quality, local adaptation, workload and talent rights.
Events & city teamsSafety and service requirements, vendors, venue constraints and escalation owners.
Engineering / design / dataSmallest release, instrumentation quality and operational support.
Partnerships / legalEntitlement terms, distribution measurement and permitted data use.

Dependencies are part of prioritisation. A high-impact idea that requires unready billing, rights or partner data may need a smaller first step.

What I would resolve before promising a roadmap

  • Which business and P&L does this role own?
  • What outcome would make the hiring manager call the first quarter successful?
  • Which teams and systems can the role directly influence?
  • What product work is already committed or in flight?
  • Which metrics are trusted, and where do teams disagree on definitions?
  • Which city / audience / advertiser segment is most strategically important now?
