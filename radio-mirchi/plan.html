What I would do in my first 90 days.

Learn the business, choose the most useful problems, make small improvements and check whether they work.

The plan

WindowWorkConcrete outputGate
Days 1-15Agree mandate; meet commercial, content, events, digital, finance and city teams. Observe customer and internal journeys.Business tree, metric definitions, platform inventory, starting result data-quality review.One accountable owner and reliable success measure per pilot.
Days 16-30Size the top two needs; validate existing work; agree estimate of what would have happened without the change and cash exposure.Pilot briefs, starting result user groups, tracking design, cost / benefit ranges, stop rules.Approve one consumer and one commercial pilot only if capacity permits.
Days 31-60Ship bounded changes; add tracking for paid access and delivery; monitor service quality.Working activation journey or advertiser workflow; weekly economics review.Results are measurable and checks that protect service and profit stable before more traffic or cities.
Days 61-90Read mature-enough user groups; close sponsor/vendor cash loops; compare rollout groups.Decision memo: scale, iterate or stop; owner, budget and next-quarter plan.Scale by extra profit after direct costs and customer value, not activity counts.

Annual Gaana renewal cannot be proved in 90 days. Use short-term activation and retention as leading indicators, maintain the user group for later renewal measurement and avoid claiming validated lifetime value too early.

Operating cadence

Analysis

Weekly business review

A compact dashboard: commercial profit after direct costs and collections; consumer activation and retention; delivery exceptions; experiment status; key dependencies. Discuss decisions and surprises, not a catalogue of activities.

Analysis

Fortnightly learning review

Show one customer journey or workflow observation, test results and what changed in the hypothesis. Include negative evidence and experiments stopped.

Analysis

Monthly portfolio review

Compare investment and returns by business. Protect committed delivery, revisit capital exposure and stop pilots that are not earning the next tranche of effort.

Dependencies are part of prioritisation. A high-impact idea that requires unready billing, rights or partner data may need a smaller first step.

What I would resolve before promising a roadmap

  • Which business and Income and cost sheet does this role own?
  • What outcome would make the hiring manager call the first quarter successful?
  • Which teams and systems can the role directly influence?
  • What product work is already committed or in flight?
  • Which metrics are trusted, and where do teams disagree on definitions?
  • Which city / audience / advertiser segment is most strategically important now?
