A proposed shared lens is incremental contribution from repeat customer relationships. It needs a business-specific definition; a sponsor, an advertiser and a subscriber cannot share one denominator.

Definitions that need to be explicit

  • Activation: a successful value moment, not an install or a login. Define the required listening or campaign outcome before measurement.
  • Retention: the fraction of a starting cohort that meets the defined value event in a later window. Keep trial, partner and paid cohorts separate.
  • Contribution: net recognised revenue minus the costs included in the agreed waterfall. State which variable, direct and allocated costs are included.
  • Incrementality: the change versus a credible counterfactual, not the number of people with a campaign tag.
  • Repeat event customer: a deduplicated person attending a second relevant edition with permitted identity linkage.
  • Net yield: realised revenue after discounts and the adjustments agreed with finance.

Three experiments worth designing properly

ExperimentPrimary testDesign caution
Partner activation journeyIncremental retained contribution per eligible cohort.Randomise or phase within comparable partner cohorts; preserve offer and season.
Advertiser package90-day repeat contribution versus current offer.Account for sales selection, account size and budget cannibalisation.
Event reminder / return loopIncremental net ticket contribution from permitted audience.Use a holdout; include discount cost, refunds and substitution between events.

Choose sample size from the actual baseline, a meaningful minimum detectable effect, allocation and desired statistical precision. No sample-size number is invented here. Predefine the decision window and guardrails, and avoid repeatedly stopping the test at the first positive result.

Honest attribution across offline and online

On-air codes, tagged links, unique landing pages, booking references and CRM matching can identify observed response. They do not automatically capture all radio exposure or establish causation. Pair response tracking with holdouts or matched comparisons where feasible, and report uncertainty.

The stakeholder-friendly dashboard

Show what was delivered, what people did, what changed versus the comparison and how much contribution remained. Keep these four layers separate so a reach metric does not quietly become a revenue claim.
