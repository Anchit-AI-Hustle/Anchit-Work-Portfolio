HOW TO TELL IF IT ACTUALLY HELPED

Did the change make listening better?

It is easy to count app downloads or subscription clicks. The more useful question is whether people can listen, choose to come back and find enough value to pay–without making the business less profitable.

THREE MEASURES I WOULD SHOW A BUSINESS HEAD
▶

First successful play

Out of people trying to listen, how many actually hear the song or show?

↻

Repeat listening

How many willingly listen on more than one day in a week? This would be my main proposed habit measure.

₹

Money left after costs

Subscription and ad income minus the extra costs of music, AI voice, promotions and support.

Always watch for hidden harm: crashes, users leaving, unwanted popups, refunds, complaints and lost ad income. No uplift or customer numbers are assumed.

How I'd check the popup fix

BEFORE THE CHANGE

Record how often users see or dismiss the popup, whether they can start music, and whether they return a week later.

AFTER THE CHANGE

Allow immediate close, stop repeated timer-based interruptions and show subscription offers when someone needs a premium feature.

Compare fairly: where ethical, use comparable groups and the same price/eligibility. If the old experience is harmful, fix it for everyone and be clear that a before/after comparison cannot fully prove causation.

For product interviewers: detailed measures and test design

Acquisition: qualified arrivals by radio, organic, paid and partner channels. Activation: successful intentional first play and time to music. Habit: weekly listeners with a meaningful audio session on two or more distinct days. Monetization: eligible purchase starts, completed payments, contribution after CAC, royalties, app fees, AI inference and displaced ads. Retention: meaningful listening at D1, D7 and D30, then actual paid renewal later. Define a meaningful play duration with analytics before reporting.

Experiment: use stable user-level assignment for eligible iOS non-subscribers where appropriate; distinguish app version, acquisition source and plan eligibility. Primary: first intentional play and D7 return per eligible listener. Secondary: incremental profit after direct costs. Safety checks: errors, exits, support, unwanted notifications, refunds and ad loss. Set duration and sample size only after observing actual baseline data.

Plain English: D7 means a user returns seven days after joining; CAC means money spent to acquire a customer; a control group is a comparable group that did not receive the new feature. These are measures to establish, not numbers that the screenshots reveal.

What to do in the first 90 days · Try an illustrative subscription model

One scorecard · four separate platforms

Measure each app and operating system separately

Four cohorts: Gaana iOS, Gaana Android, Mirchi Plus iOS and Mirchi Plus Android. The North Star proposal is weekly unique listeners who choose meaningful audio on at least two distinct days. That is a proposed definition, not ENIL's disclosed MAU calculation.

1 · Can they listen?

Eligible arrivals → intentional audio request → first audible 30 seconds. Track app version, language, source, country, failures and paywall interruption.

2 · Do they return?

D1, D7 and D30 among eligible first-play cohorts; distinguish new and returning, organic/preinstalled/bundled and Mirchi migrants.

3 · Is it profitable?

Gross paid starts are not the outcome. Analyze incremental receipts less rights, store fees, refunds, support, marketing and ads displaced, by verified plan entitlement.

4 · Is the experience healthy?

Blocked taps, unexpected player stops, login/blank screen rates, inaccessible controls, complaint trends and cancellation.

Experiment: release essential access and truthful billing fixes without withholding safety improvements, then test optional RJ rituals against an appropriate music-only control. Pre-register thresholds using real baseline denominators. A store rating is not DAU, and ENIL's 20M+ Gaana MAU does not identify the OS split. Event definitions · Priorities.

What each metric means and how to instrument it

MeasureTracking definition for a future pilotWhat it must not imply
ListensIntentional audible play, qualifying time threshold, episode completion and returning listener separately.Not app opens, guaranteed unique people or an ENIL-wide published threshold.
ReachAudience exposed during a stated period, platform and geography, with deduplication limits identified.Not views, downloads, subscribers or GAANA monthly active users.
ViewsPlatform-defined video views plus qualified watch duration, completion and repeats.Not necessarily distinct viewers or a completed mini-series episode.
WhatsApp responsesUnique opted-in respondents and genuine two-way replies, with messages, successful deliveries and opt-outs separately.Not proof of WhatsApp groups, group membership or a 3× increase in unique people.
Incoming brand enquiriesNew, timestamped, attributed incoming briefs via approved email, business form or WhatsApp Business contact; classify source and qualification.Not three enquiries because Fortune Modak had three partnerships; not sales-team outbound messages.
Closed partnershipsSigned deals, recognized revenue, delivered commitments and contribution net of production, rights, talent and selling costs.Not inquiries, proposals, promised sponsors or media reach.

WhatsApp: response ≠ membership

For new online events, use only approved, consent-based WhatsApp entry points. Track delivered → replied → qualified participation → repeat visit, with spam/opt-out guardrails. The filing does not specify that the Border Podcast operated WhatsApp groups.

Sponsorship: interest ≠ a deal

Give each series or live event a distinct sponsor-contact source tag. Track incoming enquiry → qualified brief → proposal → signed partnership → delivered → renewed. Capture brand, format, city, source, owner and timings in CRM; show missing values as unavailable.

Exact KPI formulas, denominators and attribution checks

Qualified listen rate = valid requested listens reaching the predefined threshold / eligible intentional play requests. Video completion rate = qualified completions / qualified plays under a declared platform policy. WhatsApp response rate = unique consenting people replying / successfully reached opted-in people. Qualified inbound rate = qualified attributed inbound brand briefs / campaign sponsor-page visits (or separately declared campaign reach). Proposal win rate = signed sponsorship agreements / qualifying proposals. Contribution = realized campaign revenue less direct talent, rights, production, platform, sales and moderation cost. Never divide counts drawn from unrelated ENIL campaigns.

Guard against purchased engagement, forced autoplay, duplicate respondents, unconsented messaging, undisclosed paid placements, invalid sponsorship attribution and rights violations. Set targets only after genuine baselines exist.

SCORECARD · CONTENT & ONLINE EVENTS

What makes an original series or watchstream worth funding?

Count voluntary audience return, not autoplay impressions, forced views or fake cliffhangers. No published company audience data supplies the required conversion or age-cohort baselines; set gates from real pilot measurement.

Video mini-series

Qualified discovery → chosen episode start → meaningful completion → next-episode start → returning viewer within two weeks. Split by source, OS, language and legitimately collected adult age or Unknown.

Original songs

Intentional listening → meaningful play → save or share → voluntary replay → contribution from permitted streams or licensing.

Live online events

Invited → joined the live show → genuinely participates in a vote/dedication → completes event → returns for another show. Track unique participants, not repeated votes or background loops.

Profit and audience safety

Incremental receipts less platform, performer, creator, script, recording, music/screen rights, AI inference, moderators, bandwidth, acquisition, taxes and refunds. Watch complaints, unsafe chat, crashes and rights takedowns.

Experiment: compare a rights-cleared episode with and without meaningful RJ-led participation on compatible audiences; disclose any paywall and sponsor offer clearly. Stop if the format harms audio listening or produces negative contribution after rights and moderation. Age-group evidence · Pilot roadmap.

A proposed shared lens is extra profit after direct costs from repeat customer relationships. It needs a business-specific definition; a sponsor, an advertiser and a subscriber cannot share one base number.

Definitions that need to be explicit

  • Activation: a successful value moment, not an install or a login. Define the required listening or campaign outcome before measurement.
  • Retention: the fraction of a starting user group that meets the defined value event in a later window. Keep trial, partner and paid user groups separate.
  • Profit after direct costs: net recorded revenue minus the costs included in the agreed cost breakdown. State which variable, direct and allocated costs are included.
  • Extra results caused by the change: the change versus a credible estimate of what would have happened without the change, not the number of people with a campaign tag.
  • Repeat event customer: one person attending a second relevant edition, counted once using information they have permitted us to link.
  • Actual price earned: actual revenue after discounts and the adjustments agreed with finance.

The important rates, with simple formulas

MeasureHow to calculate itWhat to keep consistent
First-play conversionPeople with a successful first play ÷ eligible people who entered the journey × 100.Use people, not a mixture of visits, installs and accounts. Define a successful play.
7-day / 30-day returnPeople from the starting group who listen in the chosen later window ÷ people in that starting group × 100.State whether you mean exactly day 7, any day in week 2, or a rolling window.
Episode completionEligible episode starts that reach the agreed completion point ÷ eligible starts × 100.State the completion threshold; report episode length and repeated starts separately.
Paid renewalSubscriptions that renew successfully ÷ subscriptions due to renew in the period × 100.Separate annual, monthly, trial and partner plans. A cancelled future renewal is not always immediate loss of access.
Revenue per paying userSubscription revenue for a period ÷ the agreed average number of paying users in that period.State monthly or annual, exclude or include taxes consistently, and separate paying from active users.
Customer acquisition costIncluded marketing, partner and incentive spend ÷ new paying customers from that spend.A device shipped or a free trial started is not a paying customer.
Radio ad-time soldAd seconds sold ÷ ad seconds available for sale × 100.Compare the same city, station, time of day and period.
Radio price earnedRecorded ad revenue after agreed adjustments ÷ ad seconds sold.Use the same treatment of agency fees, discounts and bundles.
Advertiser repeat rateAdvertisers who buy again in the chosen window ÷ advertisers eligible to buy again × 100.Also measure repeat spend and profit: one small repeat order can hide lost budget.
Booking conversionCompleted paid bookings ÷ eligible booking journeys started × 100.Track people, orders and tickets separately; a couple ticket is not one attendee.
Paid attendancePaid-ticket holders who enter ÷ valid paid-ticket holders expected × 100.Separate complimentary passes, refunds, no-shows and capacity.
Sponsor renewalEligible sponsors who book the next comparable edition ÷ eligible sponsors × 100.Also compare retained sponsorship value, cash collected and delivery costs.
Event direct profitSponsor income + ticket income after deductions + other contracted income − included event delivery costs.List the included costs. Direct profit is not the same as final company profit.
AI usable-output rateAI outputs accepted after the agreed review ÷ outputs reviewed × 100.Include review time, correction time, rights checks and serious errors when judging value.

Three experiments worth designing properly

ExperimentPrimary testDesign caution
Partner activation journeyExtra profit from users who keep returning per eligible user group.Randomise or phase within comparable partner user groups; preserve offer and season.
Advertiser package90-day repeat profit after direct costs versus current offer.Account for sales selection, account size and spending shifted from an existing offer.
Event reminder / return loopExtra net ticket profit after direct costs from permitted audience.Use a comparison group that does not receive the change; include discount cost, refunds and substitution between events.

Choose sample size from the actual starting result, the smallest improvement worth detecting, allocation and desired statistical precision. No sample-size number is invented here. Predefine the decision window and checks that protect service and profit, and avoid repeatedly stopping the test at the first positive result.

How to measure results across offline and online

On-air codes, tagged links, unique landing pages, booking references and matching customer records can identify observed response. They do not automatically capture all radio exposure or establish causation. Pair response tracking with comparison groups that do not receive the change or matched comparisons where feasible, and report uncertainty.

The stakeholder-friendly dashboard

Show what was delivered, what people did, what changed versus the comparison and how much profit after direct costs remained. Keep these four layers separate so a reach metric does not quietly become a revenue claim.
