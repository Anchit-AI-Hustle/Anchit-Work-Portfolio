GAANA IPHONE APP · WHAT WAS OBSERVED

People came to play music. The app keeps selling to them.

The supplied iPhone screenshots show a music player covering important buttons, paid offers competing with playlists, and pricing that is difficult to interpret. A repeating subscription popup was also reported. The first job is to make listening and buying understandable.

All 7 core issues, ranked by importance and business impact

Ranked from highest retention and revenue risk (P0) to visual and copy polish (P2). Every row traces back to supplied iPhone screens, tester notes or business-model gaps, not just the bottom-bar overlap shown below.

Rank & priorityIssue & root causeProduct & business impactRecommended fix
#1 · P0 · HighestForced auto-popup paywall every 1 to 2 minutes with a 2+ second countdown locking the Close (X) button during active browsing/listening.Interrupts core music playback before value is earned; drives session exits, lowers D1/D7 retention and invites 1-star App Store reviews.Cap prompt frequency (for example, once per 7 days), remove the Close delay and trigger upgrade prompts only on gated intents (downloads, lossless, ad-skip).
#2 · P0 · Trust & billingContradictory offers and broken pricing math: “Pay for 12 Months, Get 1 Month Free” and “First Month is FREE” appear alongside an upfront ₹709 charge; ₹709 displays as ₹58.27/mo (actual: ₹59.08 for 12m or ₹54.54 for 13m); Noise Buds ₹1,399 shows ₹114.99/mo (actual: ₹116.58) with undisclosed yearly renewal price.Breaks buyer trust at checkout, creates billing disputes/refunds and fails basic price-transparency expectations on iOS.Standardise one clear offer across all screens (amount due today, exact months included, true monthly equivalent and exact post-bundle renewal price).
#3 · P0 · Blocked CTAiOS mini-player and floating tab bar overlap the Monthly Plan (₹129) card, its Subscribe Now CTA, the Noise Buds “SHOW ME” CTA and Trending Songs rows.Directly blocks taps on paid conversion buttons and music rows, causing avoidable drop-off on the Upgrade and Home tabs.Reserve bottom scroll padding (~140 to 160pt plus iOS safe-area inset) across Home and Upgrade scroll views so every CTA clears both bars.
#4 · P1 · Plan clarityConfusing yearly plan hierarchy (₹709 vs ₹799 vs ₹899) and mismatched benefits: Upgrade tab lists “Unlock Music Library”, “Party Mode” and Noise Buds, while the modal popup lists “No More Ads Your Way” and “HD Quality Music” and drops the Noise bundle.Creates choice paralysis; users cannot tell what differentiates yearly tiers or why to pick one plan over another.Unify benefit tiles across every paywall surface, label the best-value tier clearly (with verified savings %) and explain any recurring vs one-time plan difference.
#5 · P1 · MerchandisingCrowded, low-contrast first-scroll promotions: two competing upsell widgets (cyan banner + “Time’s Ticking” / Noise card) push music down; cyan banner gives Dismiss and Start Now identical ghost-button weight; plain black-text CTA (“Start @ ₹799 ₹709/year”) lacks a discount % badge and uses generic stopwatch clipart instead of music visuals.Clutters the Home feed while still producing weak click-through because nothing visually highlights savings or listening value.Consolidate to one compact, high-contrast dismissible strip with a clear savings badge, a single primary CTA and music-native artwork.
#6 · P1 · Habit gapMonetising before building a daily relationship: no 1-tap YouTube/Spotify playlist import and no Mirchi RJ voice warmth or Indian daily rituals on the Home feed.Gives users no emotional or habitual reason to switch from YouTube Music or Spotify, keeping acquisition cost high and renewal weak.Lead with 1-tap playlist import, opt-in Mirchi AI RJ voice moments, daily Indian rituals and WhatsApp-joinable Party Mode before asking for payment.
#7 · P2 · Brand polishUnpolished microcopy such as “First Month FREE !!” (extra space and double exclamation) and “No More Ads Your Way”.Makes a paid subscription product feel hasty and lowers perceived quality against global streaming apps.Audit all paywall and banner strings for crisp, consistent, benefit-led brand copy before running conversion tests.

What I would fix, in order

P0 means fix the essentials first, P1 means improve the experience next, and P2 means polish the details. These labels describe priority, not proven revenue impact.

P0 · FIX FIRST · LISTENING CONTROL

Stop forcing people back into the subscription screen

The issue: the tester reports a full-screen offer returning every 1–2 minutes, with Close temporarily locked.

The fix: let Close work immediately, remember dismissal and offer paid plans only when someone requests a benefit.

How I would confirm the change

Reproduce across iOS account states, then count interrupted sessions, offer dismissals, successful first plays, complaints and later return. Churn or App Store review impact is not proven by the screenshots.

P0 · FIX FIRST · USABLE BUTTONS

Uncover songs and payment actions

The issue: the bottom music player and floating tabs overlap Monthly Plan, the Noise bundle’s “SHOW ME” button and Trending Songs.

The fix: keep the page content clear of both fixed bars, including the iPhone’s safe-area space.

How I would confirm the change

Test on several iPhone sizes, text enlargement, VoiceOver and while scrolling. Make sure every important button is visible, reachable and tappable.

P0 · FIX FIRST · PAYMENT TRUST

Tell people what they pay and receive

The issue: “Pay for 12 Months, Get 1 Month Free” and “First Month is FREE” appear with a ₹709 upfront price. The displayed monthly equivalent also does not match simple 12-month division.

The fix: show amount due today, months of access, next charge and cancellation terms in the same way on every screen.

How I would confirm the change

Compare the real iOS payment sheet, receipts, web offers and available plans. ₹709 ÷ 12 = ₹59.08 and ₹709 ÷ 13 = ₹54.54; the observed ₹58.27/month label needs reconciliation. Do not present a charge-upfront offer as a zero-charge trial without the real terms.

Then make the paid offer easier to understand

P1 · IMPROVE NEXT · HOME

Stop letting promotions crowd out music

A cyan banner and “Time’s Ticking” offer compete for the first scroll. Make the play/resume action prominent and use one clear, dismissible paid offer instead of multiple competing messages.

P1 · IMPROVE NEXT · PRICING

Explain ₹709, ₹799 and ₹899

₹799 is shown as a crossed-out comparison, not a verified third iOS plan. Distinguish an annual recurring offer from a one-time annual purchase and disclose any Noise hardware renewal terms.

P2 · POLISH · WORDING

Write like a trusted music brand

Replace phrases such as “First Month FREE !!” and “No More Ads Your Way” with clear benefits. Show “Save ₹90” only if ₹799 is a verified like-for-like reference price. Pair this with a 1-tap playlist import and Mirchi voice rituals so listeners have a reason to switch from YouTube Music.

Full quality-assurance checklist and research limits

The screenshots cannot show the actual purchase receipt, 1-star review effect or how many customers leave. Re-check trial eligibility, iOS/web prices, cancellation and restoration, failed payment, offline listening, bundle redemption, player and keyboard overlap, content rights, login persistence, notification prompts, playlist imports, accessibility labels and support handling.

Priority here means urgency to validate and repair, not estimated revenue impact. A fix is successful only when it works on devices and listening, trust and commercial measures are stable or better.

Observed findings, without overstating impact

Observed / needs validation

O1 · Mirchi-to-Gaana transition

Mirchi Reads uses Gaana navigation and subscription calls to action; the separate app landing still promotes Mirchi Plus. That is an observed mixed destination story, not proof users are confused. S21 S20

Validate: can a new user explain which app they need and preserve their intended content through the handoff? Measure: destination success and retained listening.

Observed / scoped

O2 · Mobile accessibility constraint

Mirchi Reads declares a viewport with user-scalable=no. At 390px, the first article heading appeared around 522px from the top in this session, following navigation and an ad region. S21

Validate: pinch / browser zoom and first-content visibility on real devices with and without ads. Test: allow zoom and reduce non-content space while monitoring ad profit after direct costs.

Observed / region-specific

O3 · App availability needs a regional check

The India Mirchi iOS URL returned page not found; the app-download landing links a US storefront, while Qatar metadata remains accessible. S17 S20

Validate: the intended India migration / app policy, current installation paths and deep-link fallbacks. A regional unavailable page is not evidence that all apps are discontinued.

Observed / checkout untested

O4 · Existing plan choice is substantial

Gaana web showed trial, recurring and one-time annual choices. Cancellation guidance was present in its public FAQ. S23

Validate: selected renewal amount, mandate comprehension, purchase restore and actual cancellation tasks. Presence of a choice does not prove the complete task works.

Observed / partial playback

O5 · A regional story plays publicly

Sunday Suspense Bangla series and episodes were visible. A brief play test advanced the HTML audio clock with readyState 4. S25

Limit: this demonstrates a browser playback start in this session, not full-length access, audible quality, native playback or offline paid access. Content-level access may vary.

Observed / status question

O6 · Campaign status needs reconciliation

Freshers home displayed a live campus and completed campuses, while published terms gave a tentative September end. S12 S13

Validate: whether terms were extended and ensure the displayed status, eligibility and current legal period agree. Do not label the campaign inactive solely from the tentative date.
