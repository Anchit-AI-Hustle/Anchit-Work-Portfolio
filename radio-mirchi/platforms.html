Observed findings, without overstating impact

Observed / needs validation

O1 · Mirchi-to-Gaana transition

Mirchi Reads uses Gaana navigation and subscription calls to action; the separate app landing still promotes Mirchi Plus. That is an observed mixed destination story, not proof users are confused. S21 S20

Validate: can a new user explain which app they need and preserve their intended content through the handoff? Measure: destination success and retained listening.

Observed / scoped

O2 · Mobile accessibility constraint

Mirchi Reads declares a viewport with user-scalable=no. At 390px, the first article heading appeared around 522px from the top in this session, following navigation and an ad region. S21

Validate: pinch / browser zoom and first-content visibility on real devices with and without ads. Test: allow zoom and reduce non-content space while monitoring ad profit after direct costs.

Observed / region-specific

O3 · App availability needs a regional check

The India Mirchi iOS URL returned page not found; the app-download landing links a US storefront, while Qatar metadata remains accessible. S17 S20

Validate: the intended India migration / app policy, current installation paths and deep-link fallbacks. A regional unavailable page is not evidence that all apps are discontinued.

Observed / checkout untested

O4 · Existing plan choice is substantial

Gaana web showed trial, recurring and one-time annual choices. Cancellation guidance was present in its public FAQ. S23

Validate: selected renewal amount, mandate comprehension, purchase restore and actual cancellation tasks. Presence of a choice does not prove the complete task works.

Observed / partial playback

O5 · A regional story plays publicly

Sunday Suspense Bangla series and episodes were visible. A brief play test advanced the HTML audio clock with readyState 4. S25

Limit: this demonstrates a browser playback start in this session, not full-length access, audible quality, native playback or offline paid access. Content-level access may vary.

Observed / status question

O6 · Campaign status needs reconciliation

Freshers home displayed a live campus and completed campuses, while published terms gave a tentative September end. S12 S13

Validate: whether terms were extended and ensure the displayed status, eligibility and current legal period agree. Do not label the campaign inactive solely from the tentative date.

Gaana iOS: evidence-led product and business audit

Evidence boundary: the user-supplied Gaana iOS screenshots and recording description of 23 November 2026; the web pricing surfaces documented separately. The frequency of repeated popups, actual payment receipts, App Store effects and causal churn have not been independently reproduced. P0/P1/P2 indicate priority to validate and fix, not measured impact.

PriorityFindingFix and proof
P0 · Paywall controlUser reports the full-screen subscription modal recurring every 1–2 minutes and a close (X) locked for 2+ seconds. This interrupts listening; rage quits, D1/D7 impact and review damage remain hypotheses.Reproduce on iOS across user states, remove timer-based re-entry, enable instant close, honour dismissal, cap frequency and show premium offers only on relevant user intent. Observe exits, dismissals, first play, D1/D7 and complaints.
P0 · Bottom overlapPersistent mini-player plus floating glass bottom navigation visually obstruct the ₹129 Monthly Plan card and Subscribe Now, Noise Buds SHOW ME CTA and Trending Songs rows.Reserve bottom scroll padding equal to both bars and iOS safe area, separate stacking contexts and test every CTA for visibility, tap bounds, VoiceOver and Dynamic Type across iPhone viewports.
P0 · Offer contradiction“Pay for 12 Months, Get 1 Month Free” versus “First Month is FREE for New Subscribers!” alongside upfront ₹709: the user cannot tell whether a ₹0 trial or a 13-month service term is promised.Bind all promotions to actual SKU terms: billed today, entitlement period, eligibility, next renewal amount and cancellation/restore. Do not describe a paid-upfront offer as a zero-charge trial. Confirm purchase sheet and receipt.
P1 · First-scroll merchandisingPinned cyan subscription banner and “Time’s Ticking” / Noise card compete for the first scroll, displacing music discovery. “Dismiss” and “Start Now” have similar emphasis; black-text ₹799/₹709/year price hides the saving. Stopwatch clipart lacks a music benefit.Prioritise Play/Resume and one compelling dismissible premium placement. “Save ₹90 / about 11%” only if ₹799 is a verified like-for-like reference. Clear primary CTA, visible dismissal and creative grounded in music/RJ value. Test first plays, offer CTR and paid starts.
P1 · Plan and benefit hierarchy₹709, ₹799 and ₹899 are not differentiated well; Upgrade says “Unlock Music Library” / “Enjoy Party Mode”, popup says “No More Ads Your Way” / “HD Quality Music”, and the Noise bundle is absent from one surface.Use one benefit matrix and plan schema across Upgrade, popup, bundle, checkout. Explain yearly auto-renew vs pay-once, hardware entitlement and eligibility; do not call ₹799 a third tier without a purchasable SKU.
P2 · Copy and accessibility“First Month FREE !!”, “No More Ads Your Way” and artificial timer urgency undermine clarity. Contrast, accessibility labels, cancellation discoverability and tap targets are not yet independently validated.Plain, accurate benefit copy; test price comprehension, accessibility/contrast, 44-point hit targets, legal disclosures, failed payment, entitlement restore and support flows.
Additional verification backlog, not presumed defects

App Store trial eligibility, different iOS/web SKU prices, subscription restoration, expiry and offline downloads, bundle redemption and auto-renew, keyboard/player overlap, login persistence, paywall frequency after dismiss, notification permission prompts, rights-restricted tracks, playlist import and support escalation. Screenshots cannot prove how often a bug occurs or its revenue effect.
