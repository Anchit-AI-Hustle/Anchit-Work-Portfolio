Independent business case · Radio Mirchi

How Mirchi and Gaana compete for digital listening

Gaana is an ENIL business. For Radio Mirchi, the relevant question is how listeners find and return to its shows when content travels across services.

Partner decision · Gaana controls streaming changes. A station can propose a better handoff from its own content.

THE PRODUCT IDEA · GAANA

Why would someone choose Gaana again tomorrow?

A music app cannot rely only on having songs or offering a discount. YouTube Music and Spotify already give many people easy access to music. My idea is to make Gaana feel more personal to everyday life in India using Mirchi's familiar voices, languages, rituals and communities.

This is a proposal to test, not a claim that Gaana has proved these features work. Start by fixing the listening and paywall problems seen in the iPhone audit.

The competition people already know

Find almost any song. Discover popular artists. Listen on familiar devices.

A reason Gaana could feel different

“This is my RJ, my language, my commute, my memories and my people.”

Five ideas, each built around a real listening moment

♫01 · YOUR FAMILIAR VOICE

An RJ who understands your day

Imagine: A short Hinglish or regional voice intro before a song during your commute. You can always mute or skip it.

How I would test it

Start with one host and approved voice rights, verified trivia and an optional 10-second introduction. Check whether people return more often than with a normal playlist, after counting voice-generation and rights costs. Location, mood and weather must be optional and appropriately sourced.

☀02 · YOUR DAILY RITUAL

The right music for the moment

Imagine: Morning bhakti, chai-time 90s, Friday Punjabi or songs for a haldi. You choose the moments that matter.

How I would test it

Offer opt-in, time-aware listening in one region first. See whether listeners return for that same moment, with no aggressive notifications or unwanted assumptions about them.

♡03 · YOUR PEOPLE

Listen together, dedicate a song

Imagine: Invite friends to a small “Mehfil” music room on WhatsApp and attach a 15-second spoken dedication.

How I would test it

Start with content cleared for group listening and private, moderated rooms. Count invited people who actually listen and return, not just shares or invites.

★04 · YOUR LOCAL ARTISTS

Be part of a fan community

Imagine: Follow a Punjabi indie artist or a local favourite and hear approved voice messages, new releases or local gig updates.

How I would test it

Build on True Fans Charts with artist consent, one regional fan community and transparent benefits. Look for voluntary repeat use after fulfilment and editorial costs.

↗05 · YOUR MUSIC HISTORY

Bring playlists and memories along

Imagine: Move a permitted playlist from another app and save the song you associate with a trip or wedding.

How I would test it

Playlist imports depend on supported APIs, user permission and music rights. Explain unmatched tracks; keep memories private, exportable and deletable. Measure successful first plays and later return.

When to show a subscription: after someone asks to download music for a commute, remove ads or use an eligible premium group feature–not because a timer has expired. The business must still earn more after music licensing, voice costs, lost ad income and marketing. See the first 90 days, explained step by step.

How much does each activity earn?

ENIL disclosed ₹33 crore of digital revenue in July–September 2026. Its CEO estimated Gaana at about ₹22 crore. That leaves roughly ₹11 crore for the rest of digital. Management names digital solutions as the other large business and also refers to smaller components. Individual shows, ad formats, events and partners have no published revenue split here. Earnings call, pp. 7–8

What does the login journey establish?

The public site shows an editorial-to-streaming handoff and ENIL confirms product integration. The screenshot reports being routed to Gaana during login. The public evidence does not establish a shared identity database, universal single sign-on, migration of every account or transfer of every stream. A returning user’s saved shows, payment entitlement and sign-in state need a consented account test.

Gaana Plus · what could persuade someone to pay?

Subscribers received 72-hour early access to selected Universal Music releases, initially including Badshah and Harrdy Sandhu singles. Better next-song recommendations and controls for mood, activity and genre can make a paid session more useful. ENIL reported record plays per subscriber without publishing the absolute figure. Track second paid renewal and contribution after royalties. ENIL Q2 FY27 presentation

Vivo, Noise and Samsung · three routes to listeners

Vivo preinstallation puts Gaana on a handset before a listener chooses a music app. Noise Buds R1 Gaana Edition bundled one year of Gaana Plus, moving the first renewal decision to the end of the included term. Samsung smart TVs offer a living-room player. ENIL reported over 30 minutes per listening TV user in early usage. None of those figures discloses partner fees or incremental paid renewals. Artist shows and original audio · what is actually visible?

Gaana DISCVR features emerging artists. ENIL reported over one million views and reach above 438,000. Off The Record distributes artist stories on Instagram and YouTube. Unplugged features artist sessions. Mirchi’s archive also documents original formats such as Varuthi, narrated by Ashutosh Rana, and the Tamil podcast Antharangam Unlimited. Advertiser-funded audio · a buyer can commission a complete series

Girl Effect’s Sunoh used five podcast episodes to address cyberbullying. ENIL reported more than 138,000 listens across the series. A client can commission production and distribution against a defined audience goal. A listen measures consumption. The contract fee, margin and effect on behaviour are separate questions. What does each platform do for a listener?

Mirchi creates the shows. ENIL integrated Mirchi Plus into Gaana, where readers can find featured podcasts and subscription offers. ENIL operates the businesses. A Mirchi team can commission a series and promote it on air. Placement, entitlement and billing on Gaana require agreement with its product team.

Mirchi Plus on phones, the web and in cars

Launched in India in July 2022 with mobile web, Android and iOS. Launch coverage described short videos, original audio stories and podcasts in English, Hindi, Punjabi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bangla, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam and Kannada. Its help centre lists Android Auto and Apple CarPlay. In the car, the useful task is starting or resuming audio hands-free. Video discovery belongs on a phone. Current device, regional catalogue and sign-in behaviour need an installed-device check. Mirchi Plus help centre · ENIL launch presentation · Launch language list

Three journeys to test

A commuter hears a host mention a series and wants the exact episode. A social viewer taps a clip on mobile web and wants to listen before installing. A bundled Gaana listener wants to understand the next payment before renewing. Record first-play success, time to audible playback, return within seven days and first paid renewal separately. Platform observations

How Gaana went from early leader to challenger

  1. 2011 · Listening on the webGaana let people choose songs online. Its first month attracted about 1.2 million unique visitors and 11.7 million plays. One person could play many songs.
  2. 2018–20 · Competing for scaleA $115 million funding round backed expansion. Spotify launched in India in 2019. By 2020 Gaana reported 185 million monthly active users.
  3. 2022 · A different price of entryGaana ended free streaming and asked listeners to subscribe. A casual listener could keep using rival free services without making that decision. Mirchi separately launched Mirchi Plus for original shows.
  4. 2023–26 · Rebuilding paid listeningENIL acquired the Gaana undertaking in December 2023. By the September 2026 quarter, management was discussing the cost of attracting paying users and the timing of breakeven.

What made Spotify easier to choose?

Free listening reduced the risk of trying it. Spotify also invested in local discovery. It reported that international music accounted for nearly 70% of listening at launch in India, while local music exceeded 70% five years later. A listener who found familiar music, built playlists and kept returning had a reason to stay. The paywall change and local discovery are plausible explanations. Neither explains a measured fraction of Gaana’s decline. Spotify India at five years

What does the 300 million headline tell us?

Spotify reached 300 million paying subscribers worldwide in Q2 2026 and 306 million in Q3. It reported 788 million monthly active users worldwide in Q3. India’s paid subscriber count cannot be derived from those totals. Spotify Q3 release

What alternatives does a listener see today?

The November India comparison lists JioSaavn at ₹99/month, Spotify at ₹139 and YouTube Music at ₹119. Student offers lower the entry price. JioSaavn competes on Indian music and language discovery. YouTube competes on video discovery and familiar creators. Pocket FM competes for the next episode with timed or coin-based unlocks. Kuku FM competes with serial stories and premium access. Where can Mirchi compete on its own terms?

Test a city host’s daily picks, a local-language serial and an easy return to the last episode. A national music catalogue alone gives a Spotify user little reason to change habits. Compare seven-day return and four-week listening with ordinary playlist traffic. A successful first play alone cannot justify the production cost.

What can an advertiser buy from Mirchi?

A brand can pay for access to an audience, for a creative team to make something, or for an event that people attend. A contract may combine all three. Count its revenue once.

M-Ping · buy targeted advertising

A local retailer can specify the audience and pay to deliver a recorded message across participating publishers. Software selects eligible placements. Ads may run before or during audio. A companion banner gives a listener something to tap where supported. Confirm targeting, publisher access and dynamic insertion in the current order. Measure completed listens, cost per thousand impressions and the cost of a qualified response. M-Ping · Mirchi launch announcement

Mirchi Brewery · commission a campaign

The creative team develops the idea and produces the work. Gradeup used Brewery for a series of brand films. A buyer might commission a host-led reel, a branded audio drama or a podcast segment. Price production, talent and distribution separately. Check completion, qualified enquiries and the money left after production costs. ENIL FY25 annual report · Gradeup campaign

Gaana Ad Network · confirm what can actually be bought

The supplied brief lists audio spots, sponsored playlists, homepage takeovers and mobile banners. Gaana moved to a subscription-led model in 2022. A historic format list cannot establish the size of a current free tier. Before quoting a client, request a dated rate card, eligible listener count, placement rights and delivery forecast. Protect the ad-free promise for paying listeners.

Live events with digital coverage · connect attendance to follow-up

For a run or concert, a sponsor may buy naming rights, a branded registration page, event coverage or a social challenge. Someone might see a clip, register and attend. Count those stages separately. In the ACKO example, Mirchi combined neighbourhood activity with a Neon Run. Compare sponsor revenue and ticket income with venue, staffing and production costs. ENIL FY25 annual report

Content IP can bridge attention and payment

Sunday Suspense continues to publish: the 17 November Indrapatan announcement points audiences to Mirchi Bangla YouTube and Gaana. This is evidence of an active regional franchise, not an estimate of its commercial performance. S14

Proposed test: preserve a listener’s chosen story or creator through a web-to-app handoff, resume them at the relevant destination and measure qualified listening and subsequent renewal. Keep a clean comparison group that does not receive the change to distinguish genuine extra activity from users who would have listened anyway.

Do not equate these metrics

Social followers, YouTube subscribers, monthly active listeners, trials, paid subscribers and billed subscribers measure different populations. Never use one as a substitute for another, and do not add platform counts into unique reach.

Pricing: reconcile the actual bill, duration and renewal

Reported displayed math: ₹709 “yearly” is shown as ₹58.27/month; ₹709 ÷ 12 is ₹59.08 and ₹709 ÷ 13 is ₹54.54 (rounded). ₹58.27 × 12 = ₹699.24. The ₹1,399 Noise Buds R1 bundle is displayed as ₹114.99/month but ₹1,399 ÷ 12 is ₹116.58. These are display discrepancies based on the reported numbers: investigate whether the UI is dividing a different qualifying price or term before attributing a billing error. Never treat bundled hardware consideration as pure music ARPU.
