Who uses Mirchi, what they need, and who pays.

A simple map of listeners, subscribers, event attendees, advertisers and sponsors, plus the questions I would test.

Four mobile apps: who uses them?

Read this first: Gaana and Mirchi Plus serve different listening needs. Each has an Android and iPhone experience. A store download is not an active listener. The latest available ENIL Q2 FY27 presentation reports 20.0M+ monthly active users for Gaana across platforms and 153.1M+ across ENIL digital properties. Neither is an iOS/Android split, and the latter is not Mirchi Plus app MAU. Original ENIL filing.

Gaana · iOS

Music fans who want instant listening

Observed: supplied iPhone screenshots show subscription promotions competing with songs, a mini-player covering actions, and contradictory pricing. The recurring dismiss-lock modal is user-reported, not independently reproduced.

Segment: new listeners, returning music fans, paid members and Noise bundle customers. Measure: first audible play, paywall exits, D1/D7 return, completed payment and complaints.

Missing: iOS DAU/MAU, listening minutes, retained cohorts and verified issue prevalence.

Gaana · Android

Music for commutes, language and offline use

Public evidence: Google Play displays 100M+ lifetime downloads. Selected reviews mention playback on Android Auto, unexpected downloads and recommendation mismatch. These reviews are not a current representative defect census. Play listing.

Segment: organic installs, Vivo preinstall arrivals, drivers, low-network users, regional listeners and paid subscribers. Measure: install-to-first-play, uninterrupted audio, offline success, skips, D7/D30 and renewal.

Missing: Android active users, app-version failure rates and preinstall activation.

Mirchi Plus · Android

Fans of hosts, FM streams and episodic stories

Public evidence: Google Play displays 1M+ lifetime downloads and a March 2024 app update. Selected older reviews describe login and stream interruptions; one February 2026 review describes empty screens after language selection. Reproduce before declaring a current bug. Play listing.

Segment: existing app holders, series listeners, FM fans, NRIs and people switching to Gaana. Measure: signed-in episode starts, long-play completion, repeat listening and successful migration.

Missing: current Android MAU, churn, supported-build uptime and migration conversion.

Mirchi Plus · iOS

Regional access and continuity for existing listeners

Public evidence: a US App Store listing remains accessible; previously the India listing returned an error while Qatar metadata loaded. Surfaced version 7.5 dates to April 2024. This is not proof of universal delisting. US App Store.

Segment: current iPhone install base, returning radio listeners and country-specific Mirchi-to-Gaana handoffs. Measure: country-specific app/store-to-audible-play, login and background playback.

Missing: India distribution policy, iOS active users, stream success and D30.

Audience data confidence: the company figures are reported totals, while store numbers are cumulative distribution snapshots. Ratings depend on locale and review recency. None of these sources discloses an iOS/Android MAU, D7 retention, paid conversion, CAC or revenue per user. Do not invent these metrics.

Engagement: how to distinguish reach from a listening habit

ENIL says Gaana reached record subscriber plays per user and reported 30+ minutes per listening Samsung TV user. This is not an Android/iOS average. For the mobile apps, measure the following stages separately rather than treating opening a screen as listening.

Which users should we compare?

First-time listeners, daily ritual listeners, regional/artist fans, subscribed or hardware-bundled users, Mirchi-to-Gaana migrants, and people returning after a break. Use explicit chosen languages and content preferences rather than inferring private demographic traits.

What data is needed for a real census?

Authorized iOS and Android event logs, App Store/Play Console acquisition cohorts, consented identity mappings, player diagnostics, payment receipts, royalty costs, support tickets and device-based tests. Without those inputs, quantitative OS-specific engagement conclusions remain unavailable.

Event and cohort definitions for engineering and analytics

Use consented pseudonymous IDs and events for source arrival, app open, login success/failure, intentional play request, audible play start, 30-second play, complete, buffering/stops, paywall view/dismissal, checkout, successful payment, restore, renewal, cancellation and refund. Store OS, build, country, source, language, entitlement and Unknown source. Activation = eligible new listeners with a meaningful first play / eligible new arrivals. D7 = members of the first-play cohort who listen meaningfully on day seven / all eligible first-play cohort members. Weekly habit = distinct listeners with audio on 2+ different days. Playback reliability = successful starts / intentional play requests. Keep Mirchi and Gaana event namespaces separate until identity and content migration mappings are verified.

Who matters for each buying decision?

Morning show. The commuter chooses the programme. A café buys repeated local ads. A booking code helps count responses, while the station log confirms delivery. This is an illustrative use case.

Showroom campaign. The dealer pays, prospective drivers participate and the host introduces the product. Count completed drives, the dealer’s follow-up and the contribution from repeat bookings.

College event. Students participate, a sponsor funds agreed activities and organisers manage entry. Check attendance, sponsor delivery and direct costs. Ticket receipts depend on the agreement.

Segment by behaviour before adding demographics

  • Audio: language, entry intent, content affinity, time of day, device, connection quality and paid status.
  • Events: city, group size, format, first versus repeat attendance, purchase lead time and budget.
  • Advertisers: objective, category, geography, budget, buying frequency, agency involvement and credit profile.
  • Partnership user groups: redemption, listening activation, service duration, renewal eligibility and profit after direct costs.

A single retention number can hide a strong existing subscriber base and a weak partner-acquired user group. Define the population and the moment the clock starts before comparing groups.

Discovery plan with evidence standards

Analysis

Qualitative

Interview customers who completed the job and those who abandoned it. Observe the task rather than asking which feature they want. Recruit beyond vocal fans and recent complainants.

Analysis

Quantitative

Validate event and account identity, payment outcomes and instrumentation before using funnels. Segment by channel and release version so a rollout does not look like a behavioural trend.

Analysis

Decision

Write the evidence that would change the proposed priority. A hypothesis should be easy to reject when research shows the need is small, already met or too costly to serve.
