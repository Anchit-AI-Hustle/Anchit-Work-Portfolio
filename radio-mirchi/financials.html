Read the annual and quarterly numbers on their original basis

In its FY26 presentation ENIL compared digital platform revenue of ₹112.39 crore with ₹61.08 crore for FY25, an increase of 84%. Its earlier FY25 deck separately reported ₹75.58 crore under “revenue from digital”. The labels and amounts differ. Use ₹61.08 crore when reproducing the company’s FY26 year-on-year calculation. In Q1 FY27 digital revenue was ₹31.1 crore and in Q2 it was ₹33 crore. ENIL’s annual ratio of digital to core radio revenue uses a different denominator from digital’s share of domestic revenue. What happened to Indian radio advertising?

In July–September 2026, selling radio ad time generated less revenue. Management reported a 12.6% decline in commercial airtime revenue. Radio contributed 53% of domestic revenue. Applying this rounded share to ₹113.65 crore gives approximately ₹60 crore for radio. This is an estimate. The 16.1% decline in total domestic sales covers several businesses. Earnings call

What the portfolio mix says

Management reported approximately 53% radio and 29% digital in domestic Q2 revenue; the remaining approximately 18% covers events and other business. Digital revenue was about ₹33 crore and digital EBITDA loss about ₹7.5 crore. This is a rounded management mix, not a statutory segment reconciliation. S2

Radio 53%Digital 29%Other 18%

Digital’s share can increase when radio declines. A higher percentage alone does not establish strong absolute digital growth. Evaluate growth, loss reduction and the quality of the acquired user groups together.

Cash and operating costs

September consolidated cash was reported at ₹371.5 crore. For March, the Q4 deck distinguishes consolidated cash of about ₹423.9 crore from standalone cash of ₹404.2 crore. Avoid presenting them as two estimates of the same scope. S2 S4

  • Operating leverage: station and platform costs do not fall instantly with ad demand. Efficiency can improve EBITDA before revenue recovers.
  • Below EBITDA: depreciation/amortisation, finance costs, other income, exceptional items and tax can move PAT differently.
  • Event working capital: vendor advances create cash exposure before ticket and sponsor collections.
  • Do not infer runway: cash balances are not an unrestricted product budget; commitments, lease liabilities and investment plans matter.
Business interpretation

“I see improving operating discipline, but I would not declare the transformation complete. I would track profit after direct costs and cash by business and user group, while protecting the radio engine and proving repeatable economics in digital and events.”
