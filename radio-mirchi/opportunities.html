Bet 1: distribution to retained listening

Analysis

Smallest useful release

A partner redemption journey that preserves campaign and content intent, validates entitlement, and helps the listener reach a first successful session. Reuse the existing app and subscription stack.

Analysis

Test and owner

Product, partnerships, growth, billing and analytics define eligible cohorts. Compare the new journey with the current journey, keeping pricing and partner eligibility stable.

Analysis

Scale / stop

Scale when retained contribution per eligible purchaser improves after partner and incentive costs. Stop if redemption rises but paid retention, support cost or contribution deteriorates.

Proposed metric hierarchy: eligible buyers → reachable redemption → activated account → successful play → retained listening → contribution after the service period → paid renewal. Do not call a complimentary year a paid conversion.

Bet 2: a measurable local advertiser package

Offer a limited catalogue by objective, city and budget, supported by a clear delivery plan and response measurement. Start with a category that repeats frequently enough to evaluate within a practical window. A sales-assisted workflow is a reasonable first version; self-service comes after the offer is repeatable.

  • Primary: incremental 90-day advertiser contribution and repeat booking.
  • Guardrails: collection days, deal discount, delivery exceptions, sales time and listener experience.
  • Evidence: matched accounts or a phased rollout; track displaced radio or solution spend.
  • Stop: higher gross billings with lower retained margin or longer credit exposure.

Bet 3: repeatable event editions

Put event discovery, booking partners, sponsor fulfilment and the edition P&L into one operating view. Preserve attendee consent and ticket-platform contractual boundaries. The product should support a faster, better go/no-go decision, not merely make another listing page.

  • Primary: realised edition contribution and cash conversion.
  • Customer: entry wait, issue resolution, sponsor fulfilment and subsequent return.
  • Scale: comparable editions meet economics and service gates across cities.
  • Stop: the model depends on uncontracted sponsorship, unrealistic attendance or unsustainable local workload.

Bet 4 / 5: content continuity and useful AI

For content continuity, start with one franchise and preserve the destination from social post to story page to app. For AI, observe a real workflow first: transcription, clip selection, tagging, local adaptation or campaign report assembly may be candidates. Compare turnaround and error rate against the current process.

Quality and rights are part of the product

Keep editorial review, talent permission, licensed-use boundaries and factual checks in the release criteria. A faster output is valuable only if it is usable and creates no extra correction or rights burden.
