What radio does well

A useful radio proposition combines music with familiar personalities, local context, timely information and participation. The commercial product is access to that relationship. A listener who enjoys an RJ is not automatically a digital user or a ticket buyer; every handoff still has to earn the next action.

The corporate profile lists 73 stations in 63 cities, with the listener figure on that page explicitly dated to IRS 2019. Use it to understand historical scale, not current unique reach. S7

Proposed pilot: a local advertiser return loop

Analysis

Customer need

A local business needs a credible way to bring relevant people to a store or offer, and an easy way to decide whether to buy another campaign. It may have neither a marketing analyst nor a full agency.

Analysis

Minimum product

Create a scoped campaign brief, a verified landing page or call response mechanism, a delivery log and a concise close-out report. Sell a bounded test before automating the entire sales process.

Analysis

Validation

Select a small, comparable advertiser user group; predefine one primary outcome. Use matched stores or time/geographic comparison groups that do not receive the change when practical. Report uncertainty, not just attributed clicks.

Success criterion to agree before launch: higher extra profit after direct costs from repeat advertisers with acceptable collection days, no rise in delivery exceptions and no deterioration in listener experience.

Rights and regulatory dependencies

FM broadcast, online streaming, podcasts, social clips and event music can have different rights and contract conditions. Obtain a rights review for each intended reuse rather than treating a broadcast licence as permission for every platform.

The government’s 2024 expansion announcement sets a 4% annual licence fee for the specified new-city channels. This must not be assumed to replace all existing ENIL licence obligations. S28

The station should establish how renewal exposure and regulatory changes enter deciding where to invest money. Treat potential reforms as scenarios, not approved future savings.

Know the frequency, but check the city

The September MIB list includes Mirchi at 98.3 MHz in Delhi, and at both 98.3 and 95 MHz in Bengaluru. Frequencies and brand variants vary by market; “98.3” is not the complete network map. S35

Before discussing a particular city, verify the current station, format, listener proposition and advertiser set. A second frequency should be evaluated on audience and commercial differentiation rather than assumed to be duplicate reach.
