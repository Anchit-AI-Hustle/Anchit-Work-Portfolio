The market is moving at different speeds

FICCI-EY estimates India’s 2025 M&E market at ₹2.78 trillion. Its March 2026 release reports radio down 7% to ₹23 billion, live events up 44%, and digital advertising up 26%. These are calendar-year industry estimates; they are not Mirchi growth rates. S9

Inference: diversification is commercially sensible, but an attractive category does not make every entrant or format profitable. Mirchi should choose businesses where its relationships, IP and city execution create an advantage after extra cost.

Which gaps I would investigate first

  • Start with three real tasks: find a favourite RJ or show, play a regional episode from a social link, and book a local event. Compare steps and completion on desktop, phone browsers and installed apps.
  • Compare radio advertisers in the same city, category and time of day. Track actual price per ad second, percentage of ad time sold, repeat booking and profit after delivery costs.
  • Compare streaming groups by how they joined. Track first play, return after 7 and 30 days, paid renewal, refunds and profit per customer.
  • Compare repeat event formats by city, capacity and sponsor structure. Track booking completion, paid attendance, refunds, sponsor renewal and cash received before committing to costs.

A public feature gap is a reason to test a customer task. Missing public numbers are listed as questions for the interview and do not count against either company.

A defensible position to test

Analysis

Win locally

Connect an advertiser’s city objective with a credible host, useful local content and a relevant experience. Make delivery and response visible.

Analysis

Win through culture

Use regional voices and recognisable franchises to create a reason to return. Avoid a feature race with every large audio platform.

Analysis

Win through execution

A consistent operational experience can make a format easier for sponsors and attendees to repeat. Build repeatability around margin and service quality.
