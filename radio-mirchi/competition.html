The market is moving at different speeds

FICCI-EY estimates India’s 2025 M&E market at ₹2.78 trillion. Its March 2026 release reports radio down 7% to ₹23 billion, live events up 44%, and digital advertising up 26%. These are calendar-year industry estimates; they are not Mirchi growth rates. S9

Inference: diversification is commercially sensible, but an attractive category does not make every entrant or format profitable. Mirchi should choose businesses where its relationships, IP and city execution create an advantage after incremental cost.

A defensible position to test

Analysis

Win locally

Connect an advertiser’s city objective with a credible host, useful local content and a relevant experience. Make delivery and response visible.

Analysis

Win through culture

Use regional voices and recognisable franchises to create a reason to return. Avoid a feature race with every large audio platform.

Analysis

Win through execution

A consistent operational experience can make a format easier for sponsors and attendees to repeat. Build repeatability around margin and service quality.
