ACTUAL BUSINESS ASSETS VS NEW PRODUCT HYPOTHESES

Why reach new audiences, and which segments have evidence?

ENIL's Q1 FY27 investor presentation (6 August 2026) describes a multi-city, multilingual network, a large advertiser-sales organization and custom solutions. Mirchi has also documented student, community and retailer collaborations through other dated sources. These are distribution capabilities; they are not evidence that an astrology service or mental-health bot has already launched.

Q1 FY27 · COMPANY REPORTED

Regional and advertiser relationships

ENIL describes 63 cities, 13 languages, more than 25 dialects, around 250 sales staff, 10,000+ client relationships and over 800 tailored solutions. These are organizational-scale claims, not active users of a Mirchi app.

Use case: test locally relevant shows, creator communities and trackable retailer offers.
FY2026 ANNUAL REPORT · SEPARATE SOURCE

Retailers and neighbourhood events

Mirchi reports retail partnerships, locally relevant programming and community properties, including Mirchi Back to School. This establishes precedent for merchant and educational-institution distribution, not a verified school counselling product.

Use case: shop-led listening clubs, regional perks and consent-based hyperlocal programmes.
2025 CAMPAIGN · SEPARATE SOURCE

Music and student wellbeing

The Optum x Zoom x Radio Mirchi Happy Beats music-for-wellness collaboration reached college students with performances and wellbeing conversations. It was not presented as AI therapy or a school mental-health chatbot.

Use case: professionally reviewed stress-literacy content and optional educator-led sessions.
INDEPENDENT IDEAS · NOT ANNOUNCED

Expert conversations and spiritual culture

RJ-hosted specialist sessions, vetted astrology/tarot readings for adults, family conversations and live listening clubs are proposed experiments. No launch, adoption or revenue figures have been established.

Use case: test whether repeat expert participation creates stronger relationships than passive streaming.

Which digital features had already shipped?

Use the date ENIL reported each feature when discussing a new idea. A launch establishes that a feature existed. Current eligibility, regional availability and commercial impact need their own evidence.

Before the acquisition · online stations already used Gaana

Mirchi Activation featured in the March 2006 deck. By FY10 Mirchi was distributing audio on Airtel, followed by other operators. Mirchi Top 20 extended into television in FY14. Mirchi Play was reported in FY18. By FY20 there were 24 Mirchi Play online stations operating on Gaana. FY21 reported 25 on Gaana’s landing page, including Mirchi Campus. ENIL acquired the Gaana undertaking in December 2023, years after the initial distribution relationship. Investor archive · FY21 filing

FY25–26 · reduce the effort of changing apps and finding music

Gaana reported a new Android and iOS version and an affiliate subscription campaign. Later decks describe importing playlists, an automatically refreshed offline mix, local trending tracks and Dolby Atmos support. Collaborative playlists let friends edit together. Regional charts, release reminders, listening history and lyrics search give listeners reasons to return. Web 2.0 brought its player, queue and library together. FY27 · improve discovery, payment and distribution

Q1 reported AI-personalised recommendations on desktop and mobile web, listening-history improvements, audio volume balancing and an offer engine for eligible discounts at purchase or renewal. A referral campaign described ₹200 cashback for each side. Confirm current terms before quoting it as a live offer. Q2 added further distribution through devices and highlighted premium early access. A referral should earn credit for a retained payer after reward costs. Q1 FY27 releases · ENIL Q2 FY27 presentation
