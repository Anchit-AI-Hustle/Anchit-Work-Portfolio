How to discuss existing work in the interview

Useful framing

“I saw the work already underway. My proposal builds on that investment: define the intended customer behaviour, instrument the handoff, compare cohorts and scale the profitable result.”

Do not pitch AI, a non-renewing annual plan, better recommendations, a regional story franchise or a hardware partnership as if Mirchi has never considered them. Establish what is live, what is announced and what outcome is still being validated.
