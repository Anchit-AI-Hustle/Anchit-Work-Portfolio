What the additional reading contributes

The ENIL media archive documents the earlier FM expansion. Fortune India describes changing access to FM receivers and advertiser demand for measurable outcomes. Both help explain why a broadcaster would invest in digital distribution.

Business Today on Gen Z supports testing whether younger buyers understand the value and trust an offer. Willingness to pay for a Mirchi show still requires a listening and pricing experiment. The competitive comparison links the survey, India price check and Spotify disclosures. The shared LinkedIn commentary was unavailable. Mirchi’s published Product Manager role covers consumer web and mobile, roadmap ownership, experiments, engagement and monetisation.

Research method and confidence

Research date: 21 November 2026, India time. Financial cut-off: latest available Q2 / H1 FY27 disclosures. Source hierarchy: filed financial tables for scoped figures; management disclosures for strategy; official product pages for current propositions; public observations for experience; independent industry research for market context.

No subscription or ticket purchase was completed, and no private account or internal company data was accessed.

Reconciliation and evidence cautions

  • Q2 EBITDA: domestic ₹11.26 crore / +9.5%; consolidated ₹12.05 crore / +5.9%. Different scopes explain different headlines.
  • March cash: consolidated approximately ₹423.9 crore, standalone ₹404.2 crore, distinguished in Q4 deck.
  • The Q2 deck executive summary prints digital revenue as ₹33 million, inconsistent with its 29% share. The press release gives ₹33 crore; the domestic minus existing business bridge is approximately ₹32.93 crore. Use that cross-check.
  • Annual narrative mix percentages and “digital as % of core radio” use different or unclear denominators. They are not combined into a precise annual pie chart.
  • The company profile’s 38M listener claim uses IRS 2019. Latest deck reach / event-count claims vary across slides; do not add them or invent a unique audience count.
  • Mirchi Plus store cache and live ratings differ. The live observed rating is recorded with its date rather than presented as a universal value.
  • Featured reviews include 2019-2023 and earlier years. They are not used to claim current defect prevalence or recent sentiment.
  • Mirchi iOS India URL was unavailable in this session; Qatar listing presence does not prove availability for India.
  • A live event page can remain accessible after its stated campaign period. Registration availability and upcoming status are evaluated separately.
