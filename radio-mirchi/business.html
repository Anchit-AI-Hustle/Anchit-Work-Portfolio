Who is Radio Mirchi for?

A person listening to FM can enjoy a show for free. A business pays Mirchi to reach people in a particular city, language or setting. At a ticketed event, the attendee can also be a paying customer. These audience groups overlap. The examples describe listening occasions and buying needs. Their age, income and share of total listeners would need city-level audience research.

What can a customer buy from Mirchi?

Buyer and business goalWhat Mirchi delivers and how it earnsHow to judge the purchase
Local advertiser or media agency: get the word out in one cityScheduled radio ads. A spot is one airing of an ad. For example, 30 airings of a 20-second message use 600 ad seconds. Multiply by the agreed net price per second to estimate airtime revenue.Check the broadcast log for delivery. A shop can track offer redemptions or bookings and compare with similar weeks.
Brand marketing team: become associated with a show or occasionA sponsor buys an agreed package, which might include mentions by the radio host, a recurring segment or event signage. The fee depends on the promised placements, duration and cities.Check whether each placement ran, test whether listeners remember the sponsor and track repeat bookings.
Retail brand or dealer: get people to try the productMirchi can produce a showroom appearance, neighbourhood demonstration or campus activity, supported by radio and social content. The brand or agency pays for the agreed campaign work.Count attended demos, completed test drives or qualified enquiries. Subtract talent, travel, venue and production costs to see what the campaign earned.
Brand or agency: create content people shareA host interview, branded video or short social series can be commissioned as part of a campaign. Agree on production, publication, usage rights and any paid distribution.Look at completed views and relevant actions. Deduct production and paid-media costs from the client fee.
Event sponsor, commissioning client or ticket buyer: take part in a live experienceA commissioned event can earn a production fee. At a ticketed event the organiser may also receive sponsorship and a share of ticket sales. Confirm who carries the costs and who retains the receipts for each edition.Track paid attendance, net ticket receipts, sponsor cash collected, direct costs and whether the sponsor returns.

The buyer can work through a media agency, which places or negotiates the campaign on the brand’s behalf. A campaign can contain several of the items above. Count the contract once when reporting total revenue.

What did actual clients use Mirchi for?

ENIL describes the following campaigns in its FY25 report. They illustrate what brands bought or used Mirchi to deliver. Individual fees, profit and resulting product sales are undisclosed. ENIL FY25 annual report, pages 15 and 16

  • MG Hector: Radio hosts took part in showroom drives. The product experience was the central activity. For a dealer, the useful follow-up would be how many attendees completed a drive and returned for a sales conversation.
  • Xiaomi: Its Smart Home League brought demonstrations and challenges to ten residential communities. ENIL reported more than one million social impressions. Those are displays of content; one person may account for several. Product trials and sales require separate counts.
  • Dulux: A display contest involved 20 selected dealers across five cities. Hosts visited stores and featured entries on air and online. Here the buyer’s immediate objective was to engage its dealer network.
  • ACKO: ENIL describes neighbourhood activities and a Neon Run across five cities. A sponsor could assess participation, brand recall and the cost per attendee reached. Insurance sales would require separate evidence.

Current scale: ENIL said radio accounted for 53% of its domestic revenue in July to September 2026. That is radio’s share of the company’s domestic revenue. The release does not split radio sales by advertiser category, audience or campaign type. S2

How are ENIL, Mirchi and Gaana connected?

ENIL is the company. Mirchi is its radio and entertainment brand. Gaana is its music streaming service.

ENIL stands for Entertainment Network (India) Limited. It belongs to the wider Times Group and publishes its own financial results. When you quote revenue in the interview, say “ENIL reported…” and give the period. S6 S2

Who pays whom? Imagine a Delhi café buying an ad on a Mirchi morning show. The café pays to reach listeners. A Gaana subscriber pays to listen to music. Both activities sit within ENIL’s business, but they earn money in different ways. S7 S5

Where do Mirchi Plus and Mirchi Live fit? Mirchi Plus is a name readers may see on digital content and app listings. Mirchi Live is where people can find events and campaigns such as Mirchi Freshers. S20 S12

What is difficult to reproduce

Analysis

Local relevance

A useful city personality understands language, humour, neighbourhoods and cultural timing. Treat that relationship as a product asset, with editorial judgment and talent continuity.

Analysis

Advertiser relationships

A local sales relationship can open doors for several formats. The product challenge is making offers easy to understand, deliver and measure without fragmenting ownership.

Analysis

IP and execution

A recognisable format can travel across cities, but execution depends on local demand, venue economics and sponsor fit. A repeatable playbook should preserve local relevance.

How central teams and city teams could work together

  • Central: product platform, analytics definitions, rights standards, billing rules, experimentation and reusable creative tooling.
  • City: programming, local creators, advertiser relationships, event partners and cultural adaptation.
  • Shared governance: deal approval, sponsorship fulfilment, audience permissions and profit after direct costs reporting.
  • Who can make each decision: ask who owns the customer, product Income and cost sheet, pricing, agreed delivery standard and cross-business revenue giving credit for a result.

This is a proposed working model for discovery. It does not claim to describe ENIL’s current reporting lines.

International: a local diaspora business

ENIL’s public profile describes a presence in the USA, UAE, Qatar and Bahrain. Its FY26 operating review discusses diaspora engagement and partnership / licensing opportunities. These are company disclosures, not a fresh station-by-station international audit. S7 S5

International growth should be evaluated market by market. A recognisable Indian brand can help acquisition, but local demand, rights and execution still determine the economics.
