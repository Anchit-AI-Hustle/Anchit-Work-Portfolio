Concept demo · not a Lenskart product

A clickable demo of one idea for Lenskart: follow up first-time testers, and remind those who ask when their next eye test is due.

A demo built for an outside-in case study of Lenskart, the Indian eyewear retailer, from public data only. It shows how Lenskart could follow up people after their first eye test, remind parents of children and adults who ask when the next test is due, book it, and suggest lenses or a new pair only after the test says one is needed. The six customers and all their data are made up.

Read the case studySee the evidence
  1. Pick a customer. Six people at different points: overdue, up to date, a child, a first eye test.
  2. Change something. Move the months since their last test, record a re-test result, or report trouble seeing.
  3. Watch what happens. The phone, the due date and the WhatsApp message all update, and each says why.

      Recall Reminder on WhatsApp

      One WhatsApp message when a test falls due, with a slot to book. Who receives it depends on the wearer's age, and on whether they asked for it.

      WearerRe-test rhythmReminder goes to
      Under 18Every 12 months; every 6 months on myopia-control lensesTo the parent, if the parent asked at the eye test (the DPDP Act 2023 needs a parent's consent for under-18s)
      18 to 64Every 24 months, sooner if they report trouble seeingTo the wearer, only if they asked at the eye test. A family member gets a copy only if the wearer opts in
      65 and aboveEvery 12 monthsTo the wearer, only if they asked at the eye test. A family member gets a copy only if the wearer opts in

      What each reply does

      • Book opens the slot picker with the saved profile attached.
      • I'm having trouble seeing moves the test up; within three months of a test it becomes a free re-check at the store.
      • Not now snoozes the reminder for 90 days and cancels the call.
      • STOP ends reminders for good.

      Guardrails built in

      • No price or discount in any message. A reminder is about the eye test only.
      • Adults get reminders only if they asked for them at the eye test. For a child, the parent is asked.
      • One reminder when a test falls due and a second a week later. If the test is still not done at one and a half times the interval, one more goes out and Lenskart may call once. Then it stops until the next due date.
      • Calls go only to customers who agreed at their eye test, from Lenskart's registered service line, as India's telemarketing rules require.
      • Re-tests are booked into open optometrist slots, never a first-time customer's.
      • Nothing is sent while a service ticket is open; the ticket gets fixed first.
      • No fear language and no diagnosis. Sudden loss of vision, pain or flashes are sent to an eye doctor, not to a store.

      Lenskart brand context schema

      Read from computed styles on 13 Nov 2026 with a headless browser, on Lenskart's India store locator, its Lenskart at Home page and its US storefront, which shares the same token names. From the network this audit ran on, Lenskart's India home and product pages redirected to the US storefront, so the Gold colours are taken from declared CSS variables, not from a rendered Gold page. Lenskart Sans is Lenskart's own typeface, so this prototype uses Plus Jakarta Sans, the face Lenskart renders on Lenskart at Home and on its US storefront. The Lenskart logo is deliberately not reproduced.

      Colour tokens

      Type

      • Lenskart Sans (own typeface) on the store locator and for headings and prices on Lenskart at Home; Plus Jakarta Sans for body text there and across the US storefront. Lenskart Serif appears once, on a store locator sub-heading.
      • Section heading 24 to 28px, 700 to 800, navy. Product title 16px/500. Price 20px/600. Body 13 to 16px.
      • Buttons 16 to 20px, 700, white on navy. Noto Sans Devanagari is added here for Hindi.

      Shape and depth

      • Primary button: navy #000042, 48px tall, 8 to 12px radius, no border. Outline button: 1px #737397 border, 8px radius.
      • Cards 16px radius with a 1px #DBDBEA or #F0F0F6 border; inner panels 12px and 8px.
      • Shadows are soft: 0 1px 16px rgba(0,0,0,.08) on product cards; 0 12px 16px -4px rgba(16,24,40,.08) on store cards.
      • Pages are white with warm off-white panels (#FBF9F7, #F7F2ED).

      Product page anatomy

      1. Photos, 360 view and AR Try On tabs; a "Scan Face" prompt for frame size.
      2. Title 16px, price 20px in navy, with a dashed-border offer line in blue.
      3. One navy "Select Lenses" button, sticky at the bottom on a phone.
      4. A three-step lens flow: power type, lens package, add power. Coatings come bundled in tiered packages, each priced as frame plus lens.

      Voice

      • Plain and practical: "Book Free Appointment", "Not sure about your size? Do face scan for perfect size".
      • Service promises are stated as numbers: "12 Step Eye Checkup", "in just 15 minutes", "Try 150+ frames at home".
      • Offers are loud and repeated: "Buy 1 Get 1 Free with GOLD" on listing cards.
      • This concept keeps the practical tone and drops the offer from every recall message.
