The proposed system

Vision Recall Engine: five features on top of what Lenskart already runs

It reads the eye-test record Lenskart already holds, works out when each person's next test is due, books it, and suggests new lenses or a pair only after the test says one is needed. No new stores, products or capex. Every feature below works in the prototype, and each link opens the part of the screen where it runs.

Feature 1Recall Score

How due each customer's next eye test is, from the age of their prescription, their age band, symptoms, screen time and the age of their pair. Every score shows its reasons.

For: the team that owns repeat revenueSee the Recall Score
Feature 2Re-test Plan

Overdue: a re-test booked in two taps, free at a store, and one call from the home store on day 8 only if nothing is booked. Due now, or any reported symptom: one reminder with a slot. Not due: nothing.

Feature 3Sight Check-in

One short WhatsApp message when a test falls due, sent to the parent for under-18s and to the wearer for adults. It carries a slot to book and no product, price or discount.

Feature 4Next Pair Step

After the re-test: keep your pair if the power is unchanged, new lenses in your current frame if it changed, or the right new pair. Held back while a service ticket is open.

In 60 seconds

The short version

1 · The eye test is the funnel

Lenskart ran 2.07 crore eye tests in India in FY26, up 54%, and about half were someone's first ever. Its own target: 100 million pairs a year needs 80 million tests.

2 · Strong AI, aimed at the first pair

Face scan, 3D try-on, an AI stylist whose users are nearly 3x more likely to buy, remote optometry in 934 stores, and an AI voice assistant that now answers calls.

3 · The pieces of a recall exist, unconnected

Free eye tests, a ₹99 home test, lens replacement for a ₹199 fitting fee and one crore Gold members are live. I could not find anything that tells a customer their next test is due.

4 · The bet

The Vision Recall Engine: one layer that knows when each person's next test is due, books it, and sells only what the test supports. My estimate: ₹148 cr a year of extra revenue, about ₹84 cr ($8.9M) of product margin after running costs, in a ₹36 to 131 cr range.

05 · Shortcomings

Five places the model leaks repeat revenue

In customers' own words

I read 3,500 public reviews of the Lenskart app: the 3,000 most recent on Google Play (28 Aug to 12 Nov 2026) and the 500 most recent on the App Store (29 Apr to 11 Nov 2026), collected 13 Nov 2026. On Google Play 1,946 are 4-5★, mostly short praise. Of the 1,458 rated 1-3★ across both stores, by keyword match 243 mention delivery, 235 customer support, 220 refunds, returns or exchanges and 148 power, lens or product quality (33 of them a wrong or different power); a review can match more than one. Keyword counts are a blunt tool, and written reviews skew negative against a 4.5 rating. Quoted verbatim; names left out.

What this says: the eye test is what customers trust or distrust most, so a recall has to protect the power reading before it sells anything; returning customers notice when a new customer gets a better deal; and nobody in 3,500 reviews mentions being told their next test was due.

ShortcomingEvidenceSo what
1. The second eye test has no owner2.07 crore India tests in FY26, each leaving a dated prescription on file. No reminder, recall or prescription-age message is public. The CEO's examples of a trigger are collections, such as a wedding range that "ran out of stock very fast".The only trigger a customer gets is fashion; the clinical one is unused.
2. Churn is acknowledged, not measured in public"Consumers do churn." The disclosed "2-year repeat rate ... about 98%" is repeat orders divided by first orders, not the share of customers who return. No retention or churn rate is disclosed.Heavy buyers can hide the people who never come back.
3. Tests are outrunning buyersIndia eye tests +54.3% in FY26 against customer accounts +24.2%; +44.1% against +18.5% in Q2 FY27. "100 million pairs needs 80 million tests."The more first-ever tests, the more people who leave knowing their power and nothing else.
4. Gold sells the second pair, not the next visitGold is "a second pair, lens upgrades, exclusive offers". Management plans to add "eye-care benefits and services on the app". 83 reviews mention Gold or membership; 71 of them are 1★.The fee already covers a free home eye test; nothing I found tells a member when to use it.
5. Trust in the power reading is fragile33 low-rated reviews mention a wrong or different power or lens, and 148 mention power, lens or product quality more broadly. One describes three tests in two weeks with a different power each time.A recall that leads to a wrong pair does more harm than no recall.

07 · The bet

The Vision Recall Engine: one layer on top of what Lenskart already runs

No new stores, products or capex. It connects the eye-test record Lenskart already holds to actions Lenskart already knows how to take. Five features: Recall Score, Re-test Plan, Sight Check-in, Next Pair Step, and the Growth Console for Lenskart's team.

01 · RECALL SCORE

Know who is due

A score for every customer, read from the date of their last eye test, their age band, symptoms they report, screen time and the age of their pair. Every score shows its reasons.

02 · RE-TEST PLAN + SIGHT CHECK-IN

Book the test, not the sale

Overdue: a re-test in two taps, free at a store or ₹99 at home (free for Gold); the home store calls once on day 8 only if nothing is booked, about six calls a store a week. Due now: one reminder. The WhatsApp check-in goes to the parent for under-18s and to the wearer for adults, and never carries an offer.

03 · NEXT PAIR STEP

Sell what the test supports

Power unchanged: keep your pair. Power changed: new lenses in the current frame for the ₹199 fitting fee when the frame is young, progressives where near vision has changed after 40, and for a child the lenses the optometrist recommends. Held back while a service ticket is open.

Reuses (already live)

  • The eye-test record and its date, from 2.07 crore India tests a year
  • Store appointment booking and the ₹99 home eye test
  • Free Lens Replacement and the lens packages
  • Lenskart Gold and the face-scan profile
  • The in-house CRM, the AI voice assistant and the store teams' daily assistant

Adds (not found today)

  • A due date for every customer's next eye test, with reasons
  • A check-in that books a test and carries no offer
  • A capped store call when an overdue customer has not booked
  • A next step that depends on the re-test result
  • A 6-month rhythm for children, sent to the parent
  • A holdout to prove incremental lift

Read this honestly

  • Lenskart-disclosed: 9.9M (FY25) and 12.3M (FY26) India transacting customer accounts, FY26 India revenue ₹5,265 cr, "over 1 Cr first-ever tests in FY26", 103 lakh Gold members, ₹1,950 India selling price and 64.0% product margin in Q2 FY27. ₹4,280 is my division of FY26 India revenue by FY26 accounts.
  • Where each base uplift comes from. Recall +2pp is about 198,000 accounts, 1.6% of FY26's 12.3M; for scale, my onboarding experiments at ET lifted subscriber retention 10%. First-test follow-up +1% is 100,000 buyers. Family +1% is 103,000 pairs, against 85.5 lakh India units sold in Q2 FY27 alone.
  • What I do not know: how many of last year's customers return today and how many tested people buy. Neither is disclosed, so the uplifts are applied to the whole base and must be proven against a holdout.
  • Not included: build cost, and the cases where a recall ends in a ₹199 lens swap or in "keep your pair", which is the right outcome and earns less. Figures are rounded; totals use unrounded values. Every input is editable in the prototype's growth console.

10 · First 90 days

Prove lift in two groups before scaling

Weeks 1-3 · Baseline
  • Pull two past cohorts of tested customers
  • Measure how many return, and when, today
  • Backtest: does prescription age predict the next order?
Weeks 4-8 · Pilot
  • Children with myopia and adults over 24 months
  • Check-in and re-test booking live with a 90/10 holdout
  • 20 stores in 3 cities, with the day-8 call
Weeks 9-13 · Next step
  • Next Pair Step after the re-test
  • First-ever test follow-up in Tier 2+ stores
  • Read-out and scale decision
North star

Re-tests completed within 30 days of the due date.

Leading

Check-in to booking rate, show-up rate, orders within 30 days of a re-test, lens-swap share.

Guardrails

WhatsApp opt-outs and blocks, NPS, wrong-power tickets, return rate.
