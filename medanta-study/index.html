01 · Executive decision

Make every verified patient journey complete

Recommendation: Invest first in longitudinal identity and records, reliable booking/payment and hospital check-in. Once those work, use clinician-approved follow-up pathways to improve continuity, not indiscriminate upselling.

Digital is an enabler of Medanta's hospital and adjacent care businesses, not necessarily a separately reported P&L. The FY26 annual report describes a patient app, a unified clinical record and connected post-consult workflows. [1] [26]

Discover→Book→Visit→Results→Follow-up

Key distinction: recognised identity does not establish accessible historical records. A firsthand iOS observation is the starting point of this study; no private patient identifiers are published.

What people like

Apple reviews describe convenient appointments, payments and records together and a smooth interface. Android mirror includes praise for the new interface and preventive checkups. Preserve simplicity and usefulness rather than redesigning for novelty. [4] [6]

Web and offline service signals

Reddit reports varied wait experiences, and consumer complaint sites contain appointment-delay and coordination concerns. MouthShut includes positive service feedback too. These are self-selected allegations or testimonials, not verified hospital incidents, and not all are software defects. [18] [19] [20] [24]

Review mining plan

Export both current app IDs from App Store Connect and Play Console (authorised access only); separate historical legacy reviews. Add Google Business Profile location-level feedback, support tickets, surveys and social mentions. De-duplicate repeated text by platform/date and review ID; tag journey, app version, hospital, sentiment, severity, clinical safety and resolution. Never join public reviewer names to patient records. Weekly review-to-ticket triage with owner and closure verification.

Coverage limitation: public Apple page repeated review entries; mirrored Android content is partial; Google Maps, closed social channels and full store feeds are not exhaustively available. AppRecs was available as search snippet only. No direct Android install or authenticated test was conducted.

05 · Firsthand finding and priority ranking

A profile without a history is not continuity

Proposed P1 · patient-reported

A returning patient used the registered mobile number on the iOS app. An existing profile appeared, but earlier appointments were not visible. App version, facility, UHID mapping and booking channels were not provided. This is an observed symptom, not a confirmed sync failure or data loss.

Registered phone→Recognised profile→Past appointments absent

Medanta's account guide describes Patient ID linking; the FY26 report describes an enterprise clinical record. Clinician access and patient-facing access are distinct. A legacy Android review describes a similar symptom; current Android reviews report missing/slow reports. This makes a cross-platform regression investigation worthwhile, not proof of a common root cause. [1] [7] [10] [6]

HypothesisHow to falsifyFix only if supported
Identity mismatch or duplicate UHIDCompare permitted enterprise identifier and site IDs using synthetic recordsVerified linking, reviewed merge, immutable audit
Historic visits omitted by query or filterCompare historical source records with app API response by year/status/site/channelBackfill approved fields and fix pagination/status filtering
Migration or event backlogReconcile source counts and sequence IDs; inspect lag/dead lettersReplay idempotently; monitor completeness
Privacy or entitlement restrictionTest self vs delegated caregiver and age rules with authorised fixturesClear explanation and verified access recovery
Client-only rendering issueCompare iOS/Android/web response and display on same test accountVersioned contract, caching and UI fixes

Acceptance tests

For a synthetic established patient with visits booked at desk, phone and app across pilot sites, the app displays all patient-visible visits with correct date/status; new appointments appear once; missing history shows a clear explanation and support route; retries do not create duplicates. For a new patient, empty history is expected. A shared phone does not grant access to another person's records. Never automatically merge solely by phone number.

Feature positioning is based on listings, not a comparative usability score. Medanta's differentiator should be reliable longitudinal care across complex specialty treatment, diagnostics and recovery. The patient app remains the front door; clinician and staff tools retain separate role-based access.

Acquisition and lifecycle

Publish verified specialty, doctor and location pages, clear fees/availability where authorised, multilingual FAQs and medical reviewer/date metadata. Add structured data only where factually supported; test search and AI-answer visibility with a crawl and search console, not unsupported rankings. Deep-link doctor pages to correct slots; route patients to web booking without mandatory install. At OPD and discharge, offer opt-in onboarding with identity verification; follow up on clinician-recommended care only. Use WhatsApp/SMS for generic reminders with secure links, never report content in messages. Pilot in one hospital and one high-volume specialty before replicating.

New propositions, gated by core reliability

My Care Timeline

One patient-visible chronology, next actions, report readiness and recovery support.

Care Companion

Opt-in clinician-approved recovery and chronic care tasks, escalation to staff.

Digital arrival

Verified booking and QR receipt, wayfinding, queue status and help desk.

International care desk

Secure case documents, human-reviewed estimate, multilingual coordination.

Monetisation comes from appropriate completed care and operational efficiencies; avoid subscriptions that restrict patient record access, unnecessary tests, or incentives for inappropriate procedures.

08 · Technical and AI transformation

Build trust into the architecture

Known baseline: enterprise clinical records, connected patient app and clinician tools. [1] [23] [26] Do not claim a specific deployed AI model without source evidence.

HIS / EMR / LIS / RIS→Verified identity + consent→Event reconciliation→Patient API→App / web / agent

Proposed architecture: master patient index with human-reviewed exception queue, versioned FHIR-aligned adapters where supported, idempotent booking and payment ledger, patient-visible record projections, outbox/retry and dead-letter reconciliation. Role- and attribute-based access; separate staff and patient scopes; encryption, audit logs, retention and access review. Verify identity before linking any ABHA record; use consented interoperability, not mandatory ABHA enrolment.

AI opportunityWhat it doesSafety gate
Support copilotSummarises verified booking status, drafts responses from approved policyHuman confirmation for disputed payment or access; no raw PHI in unapproved models
Care navigatorAnswers approved service/wayfinding questions and hands offNo diagnosis; emergency and clinical escalation; multilingual evaluation
Clinician-approved follow-upSummarises next steps from approved discharge planNo prescription changes; clinician signoff; patient consent
Operations predictionForecast slot demand and report delayMonitor error and fairness by site, do not deny care automatically
Review intelligenceClusters de-identified feedback and prioritises ticketsHuman review, deduplication, no inference of prevalence

DPDP and applicable healthcare, telemedicine and cybersecurity obligations require review by legal and clinical teams. Use least privilege, explicit delegated consent, purpose limitation and human escalation. A confirmed wrong-patient disclosure is a stop-ship event. No autonomous clinical triage or treatment advice in the pilot.

09 · Execution

First 90 days

  1. Days 1–15 · Baseline and investigateMap identity architecture, appointment/payment lifecycle and report publication; interview 15–20 consenting patients/caregivers plus front-desk and clinical staff. Collect review exports and support tags. Test synthetic old/new/caregiver accounts on iOS, Android and web. Owner: product + analytics + HIS + security.
  2. Days 16–30 · Repair core trustResolve verified identity defects, implement clear record states, reconcile payments and appointment status; design secure support escalation. Acceptance: entitlement tests, cross-platform parity and source-to-app reconciliation. Owner: engineering and site ops.
  3. Days 31–60 · Pilot arrival and report continuityOne hospital, one specialty; QR receipt with fallback, report status, call centre case ownership, consented reminders. Establish controlled baseline and comparison cohort. Owner: OPD, diagnostics, CX, product.
  4. Days 61–90 · Measure and expand conditionallyEvaluate attended visits, records completeness, report SLA, support cost and patient feedback; verify no safety or privacy regression. Pilot one clinician-approved post-visit pathway only if reliability gates pass. Owner: clinical governance, finance and growth.
Decision gate: no scale-up if record entitlement, confirmed booking integrity, clinical escalations or reconciliation fail. Pilot target levels are proposed, not actual performance.
