Retention lever · AI-assisted telecalling

Make follow-up a care service, not a sales call

Use consented, clinician-recommended follow-ups to reconnect with patients who need care. Human agents lead the conversation; AI prepares context, approved prompts and structured summaries.

  1. 01Prioritise due careSigned follow-up due, missed visit or unresolved care task.
  2. 02AI prepares the agentVerified context, approved script, preferred language and consent.
  3. 03Human calls patientCheck recovery, barriers, adverse effects and appointment need.
  4. 04Close the care loopBook approved follow-up, escalate concerns, log outcome or opt-out.
Contact

Reached eligible patients / consented due-care cohort.

Repeat

Incremental completed appropriate follow-ups versus usual care.

Safety

Escalation closure, opt-out compliance, call quality and cost per incremental completed visit.

No cold calls using clinical data, no diagnosis or medication changes by agents, no incentives for unnecessary visits. Verify identity, respect contact preferences, apply attempt limits and route concerning symptoms or adverse effects to qualified staff.

Long-term · clinician-led evidence engine

Learn from treatment response to improve clinical pathways

Build a structured, longitudinal outcomes registry from prescribed treatment, patient-reported response, adherence, side effects and clinician-verified outcomes. Use the data to generate evidence hypotheses, not automatic prescriptions.

  1. 01Capture responseBaseline severity, signed prescription, adherence, patient-reported outcome and adverse effects.
  2. 02Build comparable cohortsCondition, severity, comorbidities, concurrent medicines and follow-up window.
  3. 03Evaluate evidenceAssess outcomes, safety, missingness, selection bias and confounding against guidelines.
  4. 04Clinicians approveSpecialty committee validates, versions and monitors pathway changes; preserve exceptions.
0–3 monthsAgree specialty-specific outcomes, follow-up timing, safety escalation and consent.
3–6 monthsPilot registry, reconcile clinical records, audit missingness and representativeness.
6–12+ monthsValidate analyses, obtain clinical/ethics review, test approved pathway updates and monitor safety.

Standardise evidence-based pathways, documentation and monitoring rather than forcing the same medicine on every patient. Account for confounding, adverse events, clinical exceptions, contraindications, preferences and appropriate evidence thresholds. No autonomous changes to prescriptions.

Methodology: AHRQ care coordination · ISPOR–ISPE real-world evidence good practices. Observational patient responses alone do not establish causal treatment effectiveness.

What is the business goal?

Recommendation: Invest first in longitudinal identity and records, reliable booking/payment and hospital check-in. Once those work, use clinician-approved follow-up pathways to improve continuity, not indiscriminate upselling.

05 · Firsthand finding and priority ranking

What to fix first, and why

Prioritise patient identity and record completeness first. Confirm the actual technical cause before implementing a fix.

Proposed P1 · patient-reported

A returning patient used the registered mobile number on the iOS app. An existing profile appeared, but earlier appointments were not visible. App version, facility, UHID mapping and booking channels were not provided. This is an observed symptom, not a confirmed sync failure or data loss.

Registered phone→Recognised profile→Past appointments absent

Medanta's account guide describes Patient ID linking; the FY26 report describes an enterprise clinical record. Clinician access and patient-facing access are distinct. A legacy Android review describes a similar symptom; current Android reviews report missing/slow reports. This makes a cross-platform regression investigation worthwhile, not proof of a common root cause. [1] [7] [10] [6]

HypothesisHow to falsifyFix only if supported
Identity mismatch or duplicate UHIDCompare permitted enterprise identifier and site IDs using synthetic recordsVerified linking, reviewed merge, immutable audit
Historic visits omitted by query or filterCompare historical source records with app API response by year/status/site/channelBackfill approved fields and fix pagination/status filtering
Migration or event backlogReconcile source counts and sequence IDs; inspect lag/dead lettersReplay idempotently; monitor completeness
Privacy or entitlement restrictionTest self vs delegated caregiver and age rules with authorised fixturesClear explanation and verified access recovery
Client-only rendering issueCompare iOS/Android/web response and display on same test accountVersioned contract, caching and UI fixes

What a successful fix looks like

For a synthetic established patient with visits booked at desk, phone and app across pilot sites, the app displays all patient-visible visits with correct date/status; new appointments appear once; missing history shows a clear explanation and support route; retries do not create duplicates. For a new patient, empty history is expected. A shared phone does not grant access to another person's records. Never automatically merge solely by phone number.

Flagship growth opportunity · Proposed product

A digital companion for every physical hospital visit

Turn an appointment into a guided care journey. The patient should know what has happened before, what to do now and what comes next without repeated calls or unnecessary queues.

Concept · Medanta Visit Companion

Check in online. Walk in prepared. Leave with a clear next step.

Not a new standalone app: extend the existing patient app and mobile web, backed by hospital operations and verified clinical records.

Explore the interactive journey

Past · Understand my care

Verified prior appointments, signed doctor notes, reports, prescriptions, allergies, procedures and outstanding care tasks. Display dates, source, author and gaps; distinguish patient uploads and externally linked records from verified records.

Present · Guide my visit

Appointment details, pre-registration, payment status, visit pass, queue estimate, hospital route, check-in exceptions, visit milestones and staff help. No promise of zero wait.

Future · Continue my care

Doctor-approved instructions, pending results, signed referrals, follow-up due dates and reminders with opt-in controls. AI can organise and explain approved content, never independently diagnose or prescribe.

One end-to-end patient journey

  1. Step 01Before bookingVerified history, previous doctor, new vs follow-up slot and facility selection.
  2. Step 02Pre-check-inConfirm identity, consent, documents, contact details, estimate and payment status.
  3. Step 03ArrivalTime-bound visit pass; desk, kiosk or staff fallback. No location permission requirement.
  4. Step 04In hospitalLive queue status, accessible route, doctor-ready alert and coordinated tests.
  5. Step 05ConsultationDoctor reviews authorised history, documents and reconciles medication; signs the plan.
  6. Step 06Results and checkoutRelease status, payment/insurance exceptions, pharmacy or lab tasks and e-receipt.
  7. Step 07After the visitClinician-approved follow-up, reminders, report review and secure support.
  8. Step 08Next appropriate visitRebook recommended follow-up, virtual or physical as clinician directs.

Clinical continuity

EMR, lab and imaging publication, clinician-signed care plan, versioned instructions, clinical escalation and audit logs. A failed sync must show a gap, not invent records.

08 · Technical and AI transformation

Where technology and AI can help

Start with operational assistance and approved information. Keep clinical decisions and sensitive access under human governance.

Known baseline: enterprise clinical records, connected patient app and clinician tools. [1] [23] [26] Do not claim a specific deployed AI model without source evidence.

HIS / EMR / LIS / RIS→Verified identity + consent→Event reconciliation→Patient API→App / web / agent

Proposed architecture: master patient index with human-reviewed exception queue, versioned FHIR-aligned adapters where supported, idempotent booking and payment ledger, patient-visible record projections, outbox/retry and dead-letter reconciliation. Role- and attribute-based access; separate staff and patient scopes; encryption, audit logs, retention and access review. Verify identity before linking any ABHA record; use consented interoperability, not mandatory ABHA enrolment.

Collect review exports and support tags. Test synthetic old/new/caregiver accounts on iOS, Android and web. Owner: product + analytics + HIS + security.
  2. Days 16–30 · Repair core trustResolve verified identity defects, implement clear record states, reconcile payments and appointment status; design secure support escalation. Acceptance: entitlement tests, cross-platform parity and source-to-app reconciliation. Owner: engineering and site ops.
  3. Days 31–60 · Pilot arrival and report continuityOne hospital, one specialty; QR receipt with fallback, report status, call centre case ownership, consented reminders. Establish controlled baseline and comparison cohort. Owner: OPD, diagnostics, CX, product.
  4. Days 61–90 · Measure and expand conditionallyEvaluate attended visits, records completeness, report SLA, support cost and patient feedback; verify no safety or privacy regression. Pilot one clinician-approved post-visit pathway only if reliability gates pass. Owner: clinical governance, finance and growth.
Decision gate: no scale-up if record entitlement, confirmed booking integrity, clinical escalations or reconciliation fail. Pilot target levels are proposed, not actual performance.

08 · Long-term clinical intelligence

Use patient response to improve treatment pathways, safely.

  1. 01Capture responseBaseline severity, signed prescription, adherence, patient-reported outcome and adverse effects.
  2. 02Build comparable cohortsCondition, severity, comorbidities, concurrent medicines and follow-up window.
  3. 03Evaluate evidenceAssess outcomes, safety, missingness, selection bias and confounding against guidelines.
  4. 04Clinicians approveSpecialty committee validates, versions and monitors pathway changes; preserve exceptions.
0–3 monthsDefine outcomes, response windows and safeguards.
3–6 monthsPilot outcomes registry and data-quality checks.
6–12+ monthsValidate evidence; clinician committee approves and monitors pathway changes.

Standardise evidence-based care pathways, not prescriptions for every patient. Adjust for confounding, track harms and validate against guidelines.

Patient response is a signal, not proof of treatment efficacy. All treatment decisions remain with qualified clinicians; research and ethics review as applicable.
