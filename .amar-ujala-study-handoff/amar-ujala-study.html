01
The brief

Three jobs in one line of a job description

The role asks a Senior PM to "set the product direction, bring our existing programs together on one platform, and take the vertical into new areas", for schools, teachers, students and parents, from early school years to college admission.

Job 1

Set the direction

Decide what the education business is for: one-off events, or a relationship with each family that lasts from Class 1 to college.

Job 2

One platform

The olympiad, scholarship, workshops, results and counselling already exist. What is missing is one student record and one consent across them.

Job 3

New areas

Open the decision moments nobody serves well in Hindi: Class 10 stream choice and Class 12 admission, then teachers and year-round practice.

Audit · what runs today

Six live assets, each built on its own

Each has real reach. None of them shares a student identity, a login or a consent with the others. Checked on 24 and 25 October 2026 from public pages.

Class 10 to adults, parents

Disha Stars and Disha Edge

Free one-on-one counselling by phone or in person. College discovery across 386 verified profiles (80 NIRF-ranked) and 78,429 institutions from official registers, with natural-language search. Free Board Exam Planner, Entrance Exam Planner and Career Reality Check.

GapNothing carries result-day traffic into it. English by default, with a Hindi toggle. No fake-university check.

Classes 1 to 10, via schools

AUNO olympiad

Seven subjects. ₹200 for one olympiad, ₹250 for two, ₹125 for Level 2. An AI report on 50+ metrics and two mock tests per student.

GapResults stay on the dashboard for about 15 days. Contact by call, SMS and WhatsApp is implied by taking part, not asked for separately. The class range differs between the homepage (3 to 12) and the brochure (1 to 10).

Every board result

results.amarujala.com

Board results, exam alerts and government-job notices, in Hindi, on the page families already open.

GapThe page title still reads "Government Jobs 2025" and the first links are 2024 results. The visit ends at the marksheet.

Classes 9 to 12

Atul Maheshwari Chhatravritti

The Amar Ujala Foundation's scholarship exam.

GapThe scheme and the application sit on two domains with no shared login.

Board-exam season

Tanav Mukt Pariksha and awards

Stress-free exam workshops, 55 sessions across 10 zones, plus topper and school awards.

GapOffline only. Attendees are not captured into any record.

All readers

News app and education section

The Amar Ujala app has 1 crore+ Android downloads. Education is a news section.

GapA feed, not a student journey. Main content takes 13.4 s to appear on a phone in a lab test, against Google's 2.5 s standard.

Root cause. Every programme meets the family once a year and then forgets it. Amar Ujala sees the same child in Class 5, in Class 8, at the board exam and on result day, and keeps no record that connects them.

04
The six questions

Who, what, how many, why better, who pays, and the real problem

Q1

Who will use it?

Parents and students in UP's district towns, Hindi-first, on a phone, at the two result days that decide what comes next.

  • Parents pay the fees and, for under-18s, must give consent.
  • Students at the Class 10 result (stream choice) and the Class 12 result (course and college). Younger children through the olympiad, via their school.
  • Schools and teachers register olympiad students and want class-level reports.
  • Payers: colleges and universities. UP has 8,375 colleges, the most of any state (AISHE 2021-22).
Q2

What do they use today?

Relatives, a teacher, the local coaching centre, print admission ads and admission agents who are often paid by the colleges they recommend.

  • Online: English-first aggregators such as Shiksha, CollegeDekho and Careers360.
  • Hindi news sites report admission news but give no personal plan.
  • Inside Amar Ujala: the result page, then nothing. Disha Stars only if the family already knows it exists.
Q3

How many face the problem?

About 43.5 lakh families every April in UP alone, before counting CBSE students and the other states and union territories Amar Ujala publishes in.

  • Amar Ujala reaches 4.70 crore readers a day and 65+ million users a month online (Disha Stars' own figures).
  • The cost of a wrong choice is real: UGC lists 32 fake universities, 4 in UP and 12 in Delhi.
Q4

Why will this be better?

Amar Ujala already holds the three things competitors pay for.

  • The moment: families already open the result on Amar Ujala, in Hindi. CollegeDekho spent ₹126 crore (about $13.3M) on advertising in FY25 to find these students.
  • The trust: a newspaper since 1948, 22 editions across 179 districts, that can stand behind rules an aggregator cannot: a free fake-university check, payment never buys rank, verification by the product team and never the ad desk.
  • The buyers: colleges already buy print from the ad desk, so year one needs no new sales team.
  • The data: the olympiad history of a child from Class 1 makes the plan personal, not generic.
Q5

Will they pay?

Colleges already do. Parents pay small amounts. The plan itself stays free.

  • Colleges: education bought 17% of India's print ad space in January to September 2025, the largest sector (TAM AdEx). Shiksha booked ₹44 crore (about $4.6M) of revenue in Q4 FY26 (January to March 2026) alone.
  • Parents: already pay ₹125 to ₹250 per olympiad. A ₹299 priority counselling call is a test; standard Disha counselling stays free.
  • Schools: collect olympiad fees today; a paid school dashboard is a season-two test.
  • Year one, base case: about ₹1.13 crore from one season of UP Board Class 12: ₹38.5 lakh from consented enquiries, ₹72 lakh from season bundles and ₹2.65 lakh from priority calls. Part of the bundle money may shift from print the ad desk already sells, so enquiries are the truly new revenue. The number that matters most, the price per enquiry, is unknown and is the first thing to test. Change every assumption in the revenue model.
Q6

What is the real problem nobody has solved?

A Hindi-medium family has no neutral, trusted place that turns a child's results into a safe next decision, with the parent involved.

  • So the biggest spending decision of a child's education is made on agents, ads and guesswork, and a wrong call costs a year or fees paid to an unrecognised institute.
  • Inside Amar Ujala the same problem has another face: reports expire in 15 days, workshop attendees are never captured, and the result page ends at the marksheet.
  • The gap is not content. It is continuity and consent.

The idea

Aage Kya: the result-day front door to Disha Stars

Not a new business and not a new planner. It is the missing bridge from the result page, where families already are, to the counselling and college profiles Disha Stars already has, plus a paid enquiry layer the ad desk can sell.

  1. The result

    One button under the marksheet: "Aage kya? See your plan in 2 minutes."

  2. Seven quick answers

    Stream, marks, interest, budget, income band, distance, district. No caste, no exact income.

  3. The plan

    Courses that fit, exams still open, scholarships, verified colleges, and a fake-university check.

  4. The call, with consent

    An adult student consents; for under-18s a verified parent does. Only then does a college call.

Colleges

Pay per consented enquiry

Only families who asked for a call, with consent on record. Not clicks, not ranking, not data. The price is the first thing the ad desk tests.

The ad desk

Sells a season bundle

District print ads in an "Aage Kya" admission series plus a full college profile. No new sales team in year one.

Families

Use it free

Exam guidance, scholarships and the fake-university check are free and never sponsored. A ₹299 priority call is a test, not a pillar.

A newspaper's rules, built into the product. Payment never buys rank: the list is ordered by fit and a paying college gets a visible label, nothing more. Verification is done by the product team against UGC, AICTE and state lists, never by the ad desk. Colleges call within 48 hours, never resell a number and delete it on withdrawal; two upheld complaints and they are out for the season.

Roadmap

Live for the 2027 UP Board results, with a gate at every step

Dated against the next UP Board results, usually late April, assuming I start in December 2026. Every gate is go, shrink or stop.

December 2026 · month 1

Listen, audit, ship the quick wins

  • Map every programme: users, data, owners, revenue.
  • Interview 20 parents, 10 college admission heads, 5 schools and the ad desk in two districts.
  • Patch the olympiad server, slim the Foundation site, start the legal review of children's data.
Gate: leadership agrees on the result-day entry point and one shared profile
January to March 2027

Build the minimum product

  • Aage Kya on the results page, wired into Disha Edge and its college profiles.
  • College listings for three pilot zones, verified against UGC, AICTE and state lists.
  • Age check, parent consent for under-18s, withdrawal; pricing test with the ad desk; sign 30 pilot colleges.
Gate: 25+ colleges signed and verified, or launch with print bundles only
April 2027

Get ready for result day

  • An "Aage Kya" print series in district editions, with a QR code to the plan.
  • Load-test the results page; legal sign-off on consent before the children's-data rules apply on 13 May 2027.
Gate: consent flow signed off by legal
Late April to July 2027

Launch on result day

  • Plan live statewide; listings and enquiries in the pilot zones.
  • Colleges billed per enquiry; season bundles sold; weekly metrics review.
Gate: 5%+ of result visitors start a plan and 40%+ of them finish
August to December 2027

Build season two

  • All UP zones, the Class 10 stream choice and five more boards outside UP.
  • Teacher portal pilot with olympiad schools.
Gate: 60%+ of pilot colleges intend to renew; upheld complaints under 2%
2028

One platform, all year

  • Olympiad, scholarship, workshops and Aage Kya on one profile, with one consent.
  • School dashboards and year-round practice tests.
Goal: a year-round relationship with every family
